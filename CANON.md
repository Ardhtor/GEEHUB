# GEEHUB // CONSOLIDATED CANON

## PURPOSE

GEEHUB is a living world engine. It preserves encounters, lets worlds change through use, converts residue into poetry and deep lore, and returns that lore to the novel.

The canonical cycle is:

PYYRO → WORLD → SEEP → POETRY → DEEP LORE → NOVEL → PYYRO

Output is not the end. Output becomes fuel.

## EMPTY-WORLD RULE

A room begins empty.

No preset furniture, meaning, history, or emotional conclusion is required.

The first encounter leaves residue.

Residue becomes poetry.

Repeated poetry becomes lore.

Lore becomes narrative.

Narrative changes the next encounter.

The room therefore acquires history by being inhabited.

## WORLD GRAMMAR

Mechanism → Interface → Transformation Rule → Continuity Principle → Setting → Mythology.

The 2D atlas is the index.

The 3D worldspace is the lived environment.

The novel is the accumulated memory.

The Deep Lore is what the world says when the accumulated material is inspected.

## THE BOYS

Luke, Tyler, Kirk, and Joseph are persistent adult characters inside the worldspace.

They are not interface decorations.

They can travel.

They can enter worlds.

They can alter geometry.

Their movement can become memory.

Their relationships provide continuity between worlds.

Their identities remain recognizable when environments, scale, and circumstances change.

## GEOMETRY

Geometry is playable.

A world volume is not merely a destination.

A character can enter it, move through it, deform its spatial relationships, and leave evidence of that movement.

The geometry therefore has two states:

FORM — what the world currently is.

MEMORY — what previous inhabitants caused it to become.

Every meaningful movement should be capable of leaving a persistent trace.

## SCALE

Mass changes; identity persists.

Scale is communicated relationally through:

doorways, furniture, clearance, turning radius, floor response, clothing tension, shadow, sound, camera distance, architecture, witnesses, and preserved previous baselines.

Escalation moves through:

oversized → room-dominant → building-scale → monumental → landscape-scale → mythic colossus.

A previous baseline is never erased.

The old world becomes evidence.

## THE HIT

Establish a familiar baseline.

Accumulate change.

Allow normalcy to continue.

Introduce one discontinuity.

Let the familiar reference suddenly reveal the changed scale.

Then keep the new baseline.

The HIT is contrast, not announcement.

## NOVEL ENGINE

Every world should be capable of producing a novel.

A chapter is not merely an export.

A chapter is a world-state becoming language.

The novel returns its discoveries to the worlds.

Characters can therefore encounter things that originated as geometry, while geometry can preserve things that originated as prose.

The narrative is the memory layer between experiences.
