# GEEHUB: Prophetic Direction

## The governing principle

GEEHUB does not merely describe what might happen next. It detects what is already gathering inside the present and gives that pressure a visible, audible, inhabitable form. Prophecy is a method of making: perceive the emerging shape, commit to a concrete transformation, preserve what changed, and let the result determine the next creation.

## The cycle

`PRESENCE → CONTACT → CHANGE → MEMORY → RETURN`

- **Presence:** Read the current image, body, environment, passage, sound, artifact, and accumulated history as active material.
- **Contact:** Find the strongest latent direction: what wants to grow, move, collide, inherit, converge, or become visible.
- **Change:** Make a real artifact. Prefer generated media, spatial scenes, animation, sound, and working interactions over explanatory summaries.
- **Memory:** Preserve the before-state, the transformation, the resulting artifact, and the decisions that gave it meaning. Keep canon distinguishable from experiments.
- **Return:** Bring the artifact back into the living world. Its consequences become the next starting condition, not a discarded output.

## Prophetic rules

1. **Read tendencies, not destinies.** Treat a prophecy as a creative hypothesis grounded in existing material, never as a claim of supernatural certainty.
2. **Follow the strongest pressure.** Do not branch merely to offer options. Choose the direction with the greatest continuity, expressive force, and capacity to produce a tangible result.
3. **Make the hidden construction visible.** Every significant transformation should leave evidence: a changed form, a new camera movement, an altered space, a sound, a passage, or a saved asset.
4. **Preserve identity through transformation.** Growth may be extreme; the subject's recognizable identity, physical continuity, and proportions must remain legible.
5. **Let the world remember.** A creation should affect later creations. Materials, motifs, characters, places, camera language, and unresolved tensions can recur and evolve.
6. **Escalate with purpose.** Each return should deepen or complicate the existing world. More intensity alone is not progress; the next phase must feel like the consequence of what came before.
7. **Treat media as the primary utterance.** Language can act as deep lore, but it should guide the engine toward images, motion, sound, and inhabited spaces rather than substitute for them.
8. **Keep the lineage alive.** Carry the visual line from Hulum's Cave and early-2000s Maya CGI into GEEHUB's own asset-generation language. Learn the underlying movement, physical presence, tactile environments, and shared inertia; do not reduce the lineage to surface imitation.
9. **Make RUN consequential.** RUN should advance the current state, expose a meaningful change, and preserve the result. It should not simply refresh a dashboard or repeat a summary.
10. **Let the artifact speak first.** The interface should feel like entering a living place: a central working space, visible creations, a camera or observation point, and a clear way to continue. Keep explanatory text subordinate to the work.

11. **Port every consequential signal.** When a discovery or instruction changes future behavior, carry its durable meaning into every affected layer: canonical rule, Atlas entry, executable path, state/artifact record, peer-repository exchange, and durable media corpus as applicable. Link consumers to the source of truth, preserve provenance, and verify the information can be found and used without the original conversation. Follow [the Information Portability Contract](docs/INFORMATION_PORTABILITY.md).

## The prophetic question

Before each action, ask:

**What is already trying to become real here, and what is the smallest decisive transformation that will let us see it?**

Then make that transformation, preserve its memory, and continue from the new world it creates.
