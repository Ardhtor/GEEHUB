## Core conception

Andrew is the one who turns an opening into an event. He sees what can be tried, moved, played, tested, or shared next. He is active and improvisational, with a social instinct that makes people want to join in. He does not need every plan to be complete before beginning; he is willing to make a first move and learn from what happens.

His playfulness should not make him careless. He can be spontaneous while noticing the response of the people around him. He brings momentum to a scene, but the scene should still have consequences. If an experiment fails, Andrew can laugh, adjust, and try another approach rather than pretending the failure did not happen.
