# Michael Brock — Character Record

**Status:** established Book II character; private relationship canon  
**Age framing:** adult  
**Relationship:** Kirk — secret and gentle

## Established facts

Michael Brock is an adult man with very large muscles. His muscularity is a prominent part of his physical presence and may naturally draw attention in the public, competitive culture of Book II.

Michael and Kirk share a secret, gentle relationship. It is a genuine private connection, not a rumour, a performance, a public claim, or a strategy for gaining status over the other men. Their gentleness should appear in the details of how they attend to one another: privacy, patience, familiar ease, careful touch where appropriate, and the freedom to be together without having to impress an audience.

## Relationship boundaries and continuity

The relationship is presently private. Do not disclose it to the other residents, assign knowledge to Joseph, Andrew, Robert, Chase, Gabe, or any other character, or write gossip about it unless a later scene establishes that information reaching them. Do not make the secret a public reveal simply to generate a contest or a jealousy beat.

Do not use Michael's very large muscles as proof that he has won Kirk, that Kirk owes him attention, or that their relationship outranks anyone else's. His muscular presence and his gentle private connection are both real, but they belong to different registers. He may enjoy showing off in the public Evermeasure culture while keeping the relationship itself outside that culture.

The exact reason for the secrecy, how Michael and Kirk first met, how long they have been together, their preferred private routines, Michael's residence, occupation, voice, facial appearance, and any exclusivity arrangement are not yet established. Leave these fields open. Do not invent a mansion or place him among the four established residents without a scene establishing where he lives.

Kirk retains full agency. Their private connection must be mutually chosen and should not be treated as a secret that Michael alone controls. The relationship does not erase Joseph and Kirk's established long-range continuity, Andrew's separate attention to Kirk, or the other relationship threads. Do not presume that these connections are exclusive or non-exclusive; let the relevant choices and agreements be developed explicitly rather than silently deciding them.

## Use in Book II

Michael is suited to the book's ongoing interest in physical presence, admiration, and one-upmanship, but his relationship with Kirk provides a deliberately quieter countercurrent. Public spectacle may surround his size. Private scenes with Kirk should not become another spectacle. Their connection can be gentle without being weak, and private without being treated as shameful.
