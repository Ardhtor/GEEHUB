# Mr. Kaufman — Character Continuity Record

**Canon status:** provisional fictional character introduced in Chapter LXXVII  
**Role:** coach and father of Travis  
**Primary setting:** the GEEHUB training hall

## Core conception

Mr. Kaufman is an experienced, attentive coach whose authority comes from observation, patience and responsibility rather than humiliation or display. He watches movement closely, gives specific corrections and is willing to stop a set when technique changes in a way that makes continued effort unwise. His coaching is direct, but he can listen when an athlete explains that the goal of a session has changed.

His relationship with Travis is complicated by the fact that he is also Travis's father. He wants to help his son develop, but must distinguish the coach's duty to assess a movement from the father's duty to be present without turning every encounter into an assessment.

## Relationship with Travis

Mr. Kaufman is Travis's father and coach. Their first recorded adjustment is an agreement that Travis will describe his aims and observations, while his father will add corrections that materially affect the work and intervene when safety requires it. Mr. Kaufman accepts that Travis sometimes wants his father rather than Coach Kaufman. He does not interpret this request as disrespect for his expertise.
