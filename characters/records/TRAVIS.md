# Travis — Character Continuity Record

**Canon status:** provisional fictional character introduced in Chapter LXXVII  
**Relationship anchor:** adult son of Mr. Kaufman; his father is also his coach  
**Primary setting:** the GEEHUB training hall

## Core conception

Travis is an adult athlete whose relationship with training is shaped by the unusual closeness of having his father as his coach. He values his father's knowledge and wants to be taken seriously as an athlete, but he also needs room to be a son rather than a continuing performance under evaluation. His development is not a rejection of coaching. It is the gradual ability to state what he wants from a session, give an honest account of his own movement, and take responsibility for the decisions that belong to him.

## Relationship with Mr. Kaufman

Mr. Kaufman is both Travis's father and his coach. The two roles are not interchangeable. Coaching requires observation, correction, planning and safety; fatherhood includes ordinary time, family conversation and affection that do not have to be earned through performance. Their first recorded adjustment is an agreement that Travis will describe his own training aim and observations before his father adds corrections, while Mr. Kaufman retains the responsibility to intervene when a movement becomes unsafe.

Their relationship is affectionate and sometimes tense. Travis may resist a correction even when it is sound. Mr. Kaufman may need to notice when his coaching voice has crowded out his father's presence. Both are allowed to learn.
