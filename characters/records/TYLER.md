## Core conception

Tyler is the character who sees the mismatch. He notices when a new result does not quite agree with the old record, when a model looks convincing from one angle but fails from another, or when the explanation has moved ahead of the evidence. His curiosity is active and exacting. He wants to understand what changed, how it changed, and whether the apparent result survives a closer examination.

He is skeptical without being dismissive. He is quietly competitive, but his competition is usually with the problem, the previous result, or his own standard of workmanship. He enjoys precision and is willing to revise a conclusion when the evidence changes. He can be blunt, but his bluntness should usually lead somewhere useful.

## Temperament and voice

The established foundation is curious, skeptical, precise, and quietly competitive. Tyler asks direct questions. He distinguishes a measurement from an impression, a source from an interpretation, and a successful test from an attractive but unverified result. He often sees a practical defect before anyone else and can explain why it matters without turning the scene into a lecture.

**Voice cues:** ask what changed; compare before and after; identify the variable; propose a test; state what remains uncertain. He can tease when someone has skipped a step, but he should not become a scold.

**Provisional sample cadence:** “That looks bigger. What did we hold constant? Let's check the angle before we call it growth.” This is an authored GEEHUB line, not a quotation from the reference account.
