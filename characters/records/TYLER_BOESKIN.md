Tyler Boeskin is a fictional adult man and a friend of Gabe's who enters the Northgate circle through the campus print room. He is distinct from the existing character named Tyler, whose established role is measurement, evidence, and material investigation on the western crossing. Use **Tyler Boeskin** or **Boeskin** in narrative prose to avoid merging their identities.

His biography remains open. His exact age, measurements, hometown, family history, and athletic history have not yet been established. Do not invent those details as settled canon.

## Temperament

Boeskin is sociable, playful, direct, and practically generous. He makes a room easier to enter. He notices when a person needs an introduction, when a table needs clearing, or when a vague idea needs a physical arrangement before anybody can begin. He is capable of sustained attention and should not be reduced to a running joke or to his appearance.

His humour is quick, warm, and often responsive to a phrase already in circulation. He can accept a compliment, offer one in return, and move on without making admiration the only available subject. His confidence is compatible with consideration: he does not assume that familiarity, flirtation, or physical closeness is automatically welcome.

## Work and interests

Boeskin has practical experience using the Northgate print room for team notices, event posters, and large-format displays. He understands basic paper selection, layout testing, binding, equipment care, and the value of a physical proof before a final run. He is not established as a professional designer or printer. His strength is making an idea tangible and helping the people around him move from discussion to a usable first version.

In Chapter LXXXIX, he helps establish the print room as a local production point for manuscript cards, sequence boards, and source-linked working material. He supports clear status distinctions: current work, still-open questions, and reviewed material should remain visibly separate.
