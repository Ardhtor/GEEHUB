# CORPUS

The corpus is the common substrate beneath GEEHUB's archive and deep-lore readings.

`archive/` answers: what happened?
`lore/` answers: what persists, connects, transforms, or becomes meaningful?
`corpus/` binds them without collapsing either one into the other.

Graph grammar: source → node → relation → node.

Node kinds: conversation, message, asset, concept, character, place, mechanism, motif, artifact, project, event.

Relations are directional and provenance-bearing: contains, references, derived-from, continues, transforms, contrasts, located-in, belongs-to, inspires, branches-from.

Every lore node has status: canon, experiment, or unresolved. A relation may connect experiment to canon without promoting the experiment into canon.

This is a living graph, not a replacement for the raw archive.
