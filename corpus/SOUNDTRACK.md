## Role

The soundtrack belongs to the Prototype/corpus layer as an environmental input. It supplies atmosphere, rhythm, emotional pressure, and continuity for visual and narrative assembly.

`soundtrack → atmosphere → Prototype → assembly`

The linked catalog remains the source reference; individual tracks may be adopted into specific scenes or Prototype states later.

## Continuity

**The corpus remembers. The Prototype establishes. The soundtrack gives it atmosphere.**
