# Build note — FORM / Athlete Lab

The starting point was a portrait with a clear, front-facing silhouette. The build treats that image as an interface rather than a decorative card: five selectable regions sit directly over the body, and a selection changes the adjacent training content. A compact set logger then makes the choice consequential and lets the interface respond with volume and weekly rhythm.

This fits GEEHUB's visual-system and experimental-coach branches: it turns an image into a navigable state, then connects the state to a small transformation loop (select → train → record → see progress). The prototype stays self-contained so the interaction can be judged before it is folded into a larger application. The user-supplied portrait is carried with the artifact so the page remains runnable and its provenance stays clear.

The current boundary is deliberate: session values are temporary, exercise suggestions are generic, and the page makes no claim about the pictured person's identity or training history. Review can decide whether to connect the prototype to Growth Canvas / Growth Tools, persist training data, or keep it as a standalone visual experiment.

## Extension — The Basin

A second supplied image shifts the focus from isolated anatomy to body, witness, and setting. Its wide composition becomes a field study with three selectable scene readings, camera focus controls, and a local observation trail. This continues GEEHUB's embodied-scale direction: scale is communicated by the relationship between a figure, a witness, and the landscape. The scene remains a proposal, and saved notes stay in the visitor's browser.
