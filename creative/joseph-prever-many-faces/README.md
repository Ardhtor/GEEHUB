A source-grounded character exploration linked to GEEHUB's Veyrthalis / Hyperspace Expedition thread.

## What the current source establishes

The experimental expedition record places Joseph among the four travelers with Kirk. In the Digitorium encounter, Joseph calls Kirk back to the immediate moment; his touch and voice restore Kirk's sense of scale. Joseph also suspects the ancient presence knows why they came, and it answers that they do not yet know themselves. The file asks that their bond remain mutual and identity-continuous while their perceptions change.

The user supplied the full name Joseph Prever for this exploration. The GEEHUB character Kirk is not assumed to be the user or a stand-in for any specific real person; the available file does not connect them.
