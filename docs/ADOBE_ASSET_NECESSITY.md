# ADOBE / ASSET NECESSITY

Adobe is a tool.

**Asset necessity is the warrant.**

The criterion for using Adobe is not that an image, edit, design, or visual treatment would be pleasant to have. The criterion is that the current investigation has reached a point where a specific asset is necessary to continue discovering, testing, or communicating the work.

```
MYSTERY
  ↓
QUESTION
  ↓
ASSET NECESSITY?
  ├─ NO → continue investigation
  └─ YES
       ↓
     ADOBE
       ↓
     ASSET
       ↓
     NEW EVIDENCE / NEW EXPERIENCE
```

This keeps tooling subordinate to discovery.

Adobe operations may create:

- visual states;
- environmental studies;
- character continuity references;
- material and surface studies;
- compositional prototypes;
- motion/transition references;
- assets intended for later video production.

The asset should have a reason for existing. Record that reason in the experience or production notes when useful.

The governing question is:

**What asset is necessary now, and what does its existence let us discover?**

Adobe is the instrument. Necessity is the warrant.
