# GEEHUB Dual-Layer Workflow — World and Awareness

## Purpose

GEEHUB now develops scenes through two coordinated but independent layers: the world layer and the awareness layer. This is a change to the working method, not merely a new subject for the fiction. The layers are developed deliberately, checked separately, and then composed into one continuous experience. Neither layer is a substitute for the other.

## Layer One: the world

Build the scene as a complete, inhabited event. Establish where everyone is, what the environment is doing, what physical actions take place, what other people and systems are doing beyond the immediate focus, and what changes as a consequence. Preserve geography, chronology, objects, bodies, architecture, weather, sound, and established canon. Characters should act with cognitive immediacy: notice, infer, decide, speak, and move when they have enough information. The world may be expansive and richly described; action and thought should not be needlessly delayed.

## Layer Two: awareness

Alongside the external event, develop what is being experienced through the relationship's separate plane of awareness. For Joey and Kirk, each is aware of the other and aware that this attention is received. This is not a report of hidden thoughts, telepathy, mind-reading, or compulsory emotional agreement. It is a reciprocal field of recognition that persists while the two remain distinct people with independent attention and private lives.

Track what each notices about the other, how attention is returned, what changes in the felt meaning of the moment, and what remains present even when they are occupied separately. The layer can be quiet and nearly imperceptible or become central to a scene. It need not produce a romantic gesture every time. Sometimes its evidence is simply the ease with which one makes room for the other, the speed of recognition, or the certainty that a moment has been shared.

## Composition pass

After the world event is clear, make a separate awareness pass. Ask: what does Joey register about Kirk here? What does Kirk register about Joey? Is the attention reciprocal, and how can the reader perceive that without being told what either privately thinks? Does their awareness alter the experience of the event, or does it remain a quiet constant beneath it? Then compose both layers into the scene without labelling every instance.

The world layer supplies observable action and consequence. The awareness layer supplies relational continuity and the felt experience of being mutually perceived. They may influence one another, but do not force a causal link where none belongs. A major external event can happen without becoming a relationship test; a quiet shared moment can matter without advancing the plot.

## Scene record

For substantial scenes, preserve a compact internal continuity record with:
- World state: location, participants, active events, physical changes, and unresolved consequences.
- Awareness state: what was mutually recognised, what shifted, what remained unspoken, and whether the layer was foregrounded or ambient.
- Continuity: what carries into the next scene and what must not be assumed.
- New canon: only details actually established in the scene.

Do not turn this record into reader-facing exposition. It is a production aid that keeps the two layers continuous across chapters, boards, and media.

## Guardrails

Do not collapse the two layers into one omniscient point of view. Do not make Seth and Nick claim access to private awareness they cannot observe. Do not use the awareness layer to bypass dialogue, consent, uncertainty, or individual agency. Do not manufacture misunderstandings merely to create plot. Do not force Joey and Kirk to focus on one another constantly; the layer is secure enough to coexist with separate interests.

Do not let the new workflow flatten other GEEHUB relationships or worlds. Joey and Kirk's shared plane is their established canon. Other characters and settings may have their own distinct relational or perceptual systems, but these must be earned and documented rather than copied automatically.

## Operating sequence

1. Establish the scene's external world and concrete event.
2. Let the characters think and act at natural speed.
3. Make a distinct awareness pass for Joey and Kirk.
4. Compose the two layers together without repetitive explanation.
5. Record state and consequences for continuity.
6. Carry both layers forward where the next scene warrants them.

**Governing principle: the world is what they live in; the awareness between them is another dimension through which they live it.**
