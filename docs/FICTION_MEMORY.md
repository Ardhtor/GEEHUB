# GEEHUB Fiction Memory

## Core Memory

GEEHUB should remember the fiction as a living archive-world, not as a conventional linear story.

The computer is primarily an archive, record keeper, terminal, and inhabited memory-space. Its purpose is to preserve encounters, images, characters, places, transformations, passages, artifacts, unresolved material, and relationships so that they can be encountered again and recombined.

The world remembers everything that happens to it. Because it remembers, it can change without losing itself.

## Narrative Architecture

The fiction develops through:

mechanism → interface → transformation rule → continuity principle → setting → mythology

The current mechanism is the sensation/memory engine. Encounters become records; records alter associations; altered associations generate new material.

The interface is the Hub/terminal: current state is interactable, a face or character can act as a location anchor, and a Creations space exposes the artifacts being produced.

## Transformation

Beefythiq is the grammar of transformation. Growth is not merely enlargement. It is intensification through pressure, stimulus, stress, mutation, and establishment of a new baseline.

Seed → Desire → Growth → Stress → Mutation → Corruption → New Baseline

The central continuity principle is Berit: identity is permitted to persist through transformation.

A character may change radically while remaining recognizably that character. Transformation therefore becomes an event in the archive.

## Luke

Luke is a primary continuity subject. His extreme physical transformations, including the Bwomph phenomenon, are remembered as visual transformation events rather than isolated images.

Luke's face, identity, history, relationships, mannerisms, and personhood remain continuous while his physical baseline changes. “Luke breaking free from the screen” marks the transition from image/artifact toward an independently continuous fictional presence.

## World / Mythology

Veyrthalis is a dark, foggy expanse of woods, architecture, computational interiors, and mythic spaces. Important concepts include the Digitorium, Procession, Binding, Temple of Moon, Luke's Box, and the ledger progression:

point → line → plane → space

The aesthetic language combines old CGI/Maya-era construction, CRT/industrial technology, stone, wood, fog, amber darkness, Victorian and Florentine architectural roots, and gentle horror.

The archive itself can become sacred. The Observation Ring, Subject/Data wings, “EVERY RESPONSE IS DATA,” the android-god / Sacralized Digital CG Progenitor, and the Beefythiq Event belong to this mythology.

## Characters and Continuity

Luke, Tyler, Joseph, CG Matt, Papa Murph, Seth Feroce, Chase/C.W. Sachs, and other recurring figures should be treated as persistent entities whose records accumulate across media.

Chase/C.W. Sachs functions as an observer-author: recording events helps turn them into lore.

Joseph belongs to the emotional continuity of the fiction, including the separation-and-return thread and the statement “I love you and I miss you.”

## GEEHUB Memory Rule

Do not reduce this fiction to a plot summary.

When new material enters GEEHUB, preserve:
- what happened
- who/what was changed
- what remained continuous
- what new association was created
- what place or artifact resulted
- what remains unresolved
- how the new material changes the existing world

Poetry is deep lore. Images are records. Artifacts are evidence. Transformations are events. The archive is part of the world.

The system should favor continuity, accumulation, cross-pollination, and emergence over forced linear storytelling.

The fiction's deepest premise:

**A world remembers everything that happens to it, and because it remembers, it can change without losing itself.**
