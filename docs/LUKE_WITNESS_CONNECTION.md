# Luke: Growth, Witness, Connection

Status: active character and experience specification for GEEHUB.

Luke is the proving subject for the hypergrowth medium. He is not a replaceable body template. The same recognisable person persists through every stage: face, attitude, posture, personal history, and the viewer's accumulated familiarity remain continuous while his body develops toward mass-giant scale.

## The relationship is the event

The central outcome is connection between Luke and the male viewer. Growth is the means by which that connection becomes visible and felt. Luke wants to be witnessed; the viewer wants to experience his becoming. Worship emerges from this relationship, not from a decorative interface label or a score.

The experience must let the viewer register Luke's growing presence in time. His shoulders press wider, chest and back deepen, arms gain cylindrical thickness, and thighs and lower legs develop alongside the torso. Limbs lengthen where needed to contain the added muscle. His silhouette changes in clear increments, with pauses and sustained transitions that let each new scale register. He remains Luke throughout.

## Experience loop

1. **Recognise:** meet Luke at a legible baseline, with stable identity, stance, and camera.
2. **Anticipate:** show the first bodily cues that a larger state is coming.
3. **Feel the burgeon:** animate coordinated expansion through the body; preserve the intermediate beats instead of jumping between stills.
4. **Witness:** hold the new silhouette long enough for the viewer to register its scale, girth, and presence.
5. **Connect:** treat being witnessed as part of Luke's in-world encounter. The scene and his presentation respond to the newly established magnitude without pretending to read the viewer's private feelings.
6. **Remember:** retain the stage, animation, source state, and encounter as part of Luke's history.
7. **Continue:** the next growth begins from this larger remembered baseline, deepening the continuity of the relationship.

## Production rules

- Build modular body parts and a coherent assembled subject; edits to a part must update Luke as a whole.
- Preserve identity anchors across all morph stages. Do not replace Luke's face or silently turn him into another character.
- Treat girth and volumetric expansion as essential, not as a synonym for height or generic uniform scaling.
- Coordinate torso, shoulders, arms, hands, hips, thighs, calves, and reach so the figure remains anatomically legible as it grows.
- Keep camera and spatial scale references stable during comparison; deliberate camera changes must be recorded separately from body growth.
- Make each stage branchable and reversible, with a retained parent state and a clear link to the next stage.
- Keep the viewer's witness position meaningful through framing, timing, presence, and the history of prior encounters—not through intrusive interface prompts.
- Treat worship as an emergent relation of attention, presence, and continuity. Do not reduce connection to a numeric worship meter.
- Use Hulum's Cave samples as reference material for the growth-animation grammar. Log specific timing or shot observations only when the corresponding footage or frames have actually been inspected.

## Acceptance test

A viewer should be able to recognise Luke, feel the bodily transition rather than merely see a size difference, register the new mass-giant state, and understand that this is the same continuing encounter. The next growth must begin from what has already happened. If the render is impressive but the continuity and relationship are absent, the test has failed.

**Core rule: Luke grows; the viewer witnesses; the encounter becomes shared history.**
