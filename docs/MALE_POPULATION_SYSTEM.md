# GEEHUB Male Population System

Status: canon baseline  
Scope: adult male characters, simulation, narrative, animation and asset generation.

## Governing premise

GEEHUB contains a population of intelligent, physically substantial adult men. They are not interchangeable body presets or a row of isolated protagonists. Each man is an individual whose body, mind, habits, history and relationships remain recognizable as the world changes around him.

**Population rule:** individuality before uniformity; continuity before escalation; intelligence expressed through action.

## The Sacred Corridor

The Sacred Corridor is the central passage through which the population encounters itself and changes. It is at once a physical place, a social institution, a narrative spine and a continuous growth mechanism. Men enter with distinct identities; they meet, observe, compete, teach, build, inherit and transform. The corridor records what passes through it.

It should feel inhabited and consequential: monumental architecture, long sightlines, thresholds, adjoining chambers, workspaces, training spaces, family rooms and places of quiet observation. It is not a sterile menu or a linear level-selection screen. Its rooms are produced by the lives and activities of the men who use it.

The corridor is sacred because continuity is protected there. Every encounter can leave a trace; no man's history is casually overwritten. Its architecture can expand as the population and its accumulated history expand.

## Character record

Every persistent male character should carry these fields:

- `identity`: stable name, age (18+), face landmarks, voice, origin and personal history.
- `cognitive_signature`: what he notices first, how he reasons, what he knows, what he misunderstands, and how he makes decisions.
- `competence`: practical skills, intellectual disciplines, craft, technical knowledge and learned expertise. Avoid making every man generically omniscient.
- `temperament`: confidence, reserve, humor, patience, competitiveness, generosity, vanity and limits.
- `physique`: height, frame, proportions, muscle distribution, posture, skin, hair and other stable visual traits.
- `movement_signature`: gait, balance, gesture, breathing, weight transfer, habitual stance and characteristic motion.
- `presentation`: clothing, grooming, personal objects and context-appropriate adult expression.
- `relationships`: kinship, friendship, mentorship, rivalry, attraction, alliances and unresolved tensions.
- `continuity_state`: current appearance, age, injuries, skills, relationships and last known narrative state.
- `growth_history`: dated changes with causes, intermediate states and reversible/irreversible effects.

## Population dynamics

The population develops through encounters, not arbitrary stat inflation.

1. **Encounter:** two or more men meet through work, kinship, competition, travel, shared interests or conflict.
2. **Observation:** each notices different details according to his cognitive signature.
3. **Exchange:** knowledge, technique, resources, stories or influence move between them.
4. **Response:** each decides what to adopt, reject, challenge or reinterpret.
5. **Consequence:** relationships, abilities, reputation, projects or physical training change over time.
6. **Record:** the event updates character continuity and becomes material for later scenes, books and simulations.

Lineage is a major continuity mechanism: fathers, sons, brothers, cousins, uncles, mentors and heirs transmit traits, skills, habits, expectations and rivalries. Inheritance is never a perfect copy; every successor reinterprets what he receives.

## Physique and animation rules

- Keep adult anatomy coherent and individual. Preserve recognizable face, stature, limb length, joint placement, mass distribution and skin/material response across shots.
- Muscularity can be pronounced and stylized, but growth must follow a readable anatomical path. Do not solve every change by scaling the whole body.
- **Luke's growth is unbounded.** Stage four is his current recorded stage, not a ceiling, final form or maximum. The system must permit continued growth beyond stage four without skipping intermediate states or losing identity, anatomy, proportion, movement quality or visual continuity. Future stages are discovered and recorded as they occur; do not invent a terminal stage.
- Growth is cumulative and stateful: preserve each prior stage, the transition that produced the next one, and the visual/physical differences between them. Growth may change mass and proportion, but must not erase Luke's recognizable face or turn every body part into uniform scale inflation.
- Animate weight, momentum, balance, muscle response, breathing and follow-through. A pose is not a substitute for motion.
- Preserve wardrobe fit and cloth behavior as the body moves. Treat clothing, props and environment as separate modular assets.
- Adult sensuality and erotic confidence may be part of characterization and presentation. All sexualized characters must be unambiguously adults; real-person likeness requires appropriate permission. This baseline does not override the restrictions of any external generation or rendering service.

## Intelligence rules

Intelligence is character-specific and observable. Show it through problem framing, technical work, memory, inference, planning, teaching, negotiation, invention and the ability to revise a belief. Give each character domains of expertise and genuine blind spots. Do not equate intelligence with exposition or constant verbal cleverness.

## Generation contract

When a prompt introduces a new man, infer a coherent adult character from the available context and create a persistent record rather than asking for a full questionnaire. Preserve established identity when revising an existing character. If details are missing, make conservative choices and mark them as provisional.

When generating a scene or video:
- select the relevant characters and load their continuity records;
- establish a clear physical action and spatial relationship;
- preserve identity and proportions across frames;
- let intelligence and personality affect the action;
- carry consequences into the next scene or record.

When generating modular assets, default to separable components: body parts, clothing, props, equipment and environment pieces. Generate a complete scene only when explicitly requested or when the scene itself is the required output.

## Canon / experiment separation

Stable identity, lineage, established relationships and confirmed history belong to canon. Alternate physiques, speculative transformations, discarded designs and one-off simulations remain experiments until deliberately promoted. Never silently overwrite a character's established continuity.

## Initial population roles

Use these as distinct starting patterns, not rigid classes:

- **The builder:** thinks in systems, materials and workable sequences; proves ideas by making them.
- **The scholar-engineer:** connects abstract models to physical mechanisms; careful, exacting and capable of uncertainty.
- **The tactician:** reads people, incentives and timing; excels at negotiation and competition but can misread intimacy.
- **The craftsman-athlete:** understands force, leverage, recovery and technique through embodied practice; observant rather than merely strong.
- **The patriarch:** carries institutional and family memory; authority is earned, contested and eventually inherited or revised.
- **The wanderer:** gathers knowledge from unfamiliar places; adaptable, independent and difficult to categorize.

A character may combine roles, but should not collapse into a generic ideal. The population becomes compelling through differences and the consequences of sustained contact.
