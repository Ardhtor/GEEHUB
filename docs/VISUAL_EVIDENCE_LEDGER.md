## Evidence discipline

1. Keep original sources unchanged and separately preserved.
2. Identify the exact source, frame, or timecode behind each observation whenever possible.
3. Do not describe an inference as something visibly established.
4. Do not fill gaps from memory when the source is unavailable.
5. Keep conflicting observations visible until there is sufficient evidence to resolve them.
6. A pass may add nothing. Do not manufacture discoveries to make the ledger appear active.
7. Derivatives must point back to their sources and state what they preserve, infer, or introduce.
8. New canonical material requires explicit approval; repetition alone does not confer canon status.

The ledger is a continuity instrument, not a scorecard. Its purpose is to keep the source available, the observations modest, and the accumulated evidence dependable.

**Return gently. Record faithfully. Leave the source intact.**
