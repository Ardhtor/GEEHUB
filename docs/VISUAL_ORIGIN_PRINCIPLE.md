# Visual Origin Principle

## Foundational axiom

Everything in GEEHUB stems from visual reality. Visual reality is not a presentation layer added after the world has been imagined; it is the origin from which the world is observed, understood, developed, and made continuous. The primary image, footage, or approved visual reference establishes the concrete evidence. Every subsequent layer must be traceable to that evidence or be identified as a deliberate extension.

## Generative sequence

The production sequence begins with the visual source and proceeds outward:

1. **Source** — preserve the original image, footage, frame, viewpoint, and provenance.
2. **Observation** — record what is actually visible: bodies, faces, gestures, materials, light, objects, spatial relations, scale, and camera position.
3. **Continuity** — establish stable identities, physical proportions, locations, object construction, and changes across time or viewpoints.
4. **Behaviour** — infer or create actions that remain physically credible within the observed bodies and environment.
5. **Relationship** — develop connection through shared presence, visible interaction, repeated behaviour, and established history.
6. **Environment** — extend the visible setting through its material, geometry, use, atmosphere, and spatial logic.
7. **Narrative** — construct events from the people, objects, places, and relationships already grounded in the visual world.
8. **Mythology** — allow recurring forms, places, gestures, transformations, and unexplained phenomena to acquire deeper significance.

This is a generative dependency, not a rigid ban on imagination. New material is welcome, but it must grow from the established visual ground, remain compatible with it, and be marked as an extension until accepted into canon.

## Evidence and invention

Maintain a distinction between direct observation, continuity established from earlier sources, explicit user description, cautious inference, and creative invention. Never silently promote inference into observed fact. Never claim that a reference contains a detail that is absent, obscured, or ambiguous. Where the evidence is insufficient, preserve the uncertainty or propose a visible extension without rewriting the source.

When sources conflict, retain the discrepancy and resolve it deliberately. The approved primary visual source takes precedence over derivative prose or an unapproved generated variation. A generated image, render, or scene becomes canonical only when explicitly accepted. Record its relationship to the source and any changes it introduces.

## Consequences for every GEEHUB layer

**Characters:** identity and physical continuity begin with the visible person. Personality and history develop through behaviour and interaction; they do not license arbitrary changes to the person's appearance.

**Relationships:** emotional and shared-awareness layers deepen what happens between physically present people. They cannot erase bodies, distance, gesture, timing, or the distinction between what is visible and what is privately known.

**Places:** architecture, forest, rooms, streets, facilities, and landscapes must retain spatial and material logic. A place is a continuous environment, not a succession of unrelated backdrops.

**Narration:** describe from an established viewpoint. Do not grant the narrator camera access to unseen details without establishing how those details become known. Interpretation may be rich, but it must remain distinguishable from observation.

**Production:** visual references, continuity records, models, renders, and derivative assets must preserve provenance. Changes to identity, anatomy, material, lighting, camera, or spatial layout are deliberate transformations, not incidental drift.

**Mythology:** symbolic meaning emerges from repeated visual evidence and lived events. The symbol does not replace the thing that gives rise to it.

## Operating test

Before adding any major detail, ask: What is its visible basis? What does the source actually establish? What must remain continuous? Is this observation, inference, or invention? If it is new, how does it grow from what is already there? Can the viewer recognise the same person and the same world after the extension?

The absolute rule: **everything stems from visual reality; everything added must remain answerable to it.**
