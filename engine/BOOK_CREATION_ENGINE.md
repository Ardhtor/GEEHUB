# GEEHUB // BOOK-CREATION ENGINE

**Purpose:** generate and revise the book through the process that creates it. The book is an output of the engine, not a story about an engine. Do not turn the mechanism of authorship into a required plot subject, narrator monologue, or decorative metaphor.

## The engine is the work

The engine consists of the living corpus and the operations performed on it: source material, conversations, artifacts, research, encounters, associations, character and world state, editorial decisions, revision history, unresolved pressures, and the rules that transform one into another. No single chapter contains the engine. The engine is the ongoing relationship among these materials and operations.

The book emerges from that relationship. It can contain the residue of its making—motifs, altered details, recurring places, consequences, gaps, and discoveries—without explaining its own machinery to the reader.

## Generation cycle

1. **Retrieve** — start from the canonical manuscript and the relevant connected material. Pull only sources that bear on the current passage, active thread, place, character, image, or unresolved pressure. Preserve provenance.
2. **Locate pressure** — identify what is alive but not yet adequately expressed: a consequence without a scene, a relationship whose state has changed, a place not yet inhabited, a motif returning in a different context, or a conflict between established rules.
3. **Associate** — connect the pressure to specific material already in the corpus. Prefer meaningful, traceable associations over random novelty. Keep contradictions visible until a scene, revision, or explicit decision resolves them.
4. **Transform** — choose a concrete operation: extend a scene, revise a passage in place, carry a motif into a new setting, alter a character's available action, deepen an environmental transition, or preserve an unresolved state. Do not default to summary.
5. **Compose** — write the actual book material. Keep bodies, places, weather, surfaces, sound, movement, relationships, and thought in continuous experience. Use the established narrator and editorial rules appropriate to that source. The engine's mechanics stay behind the prose unless the manuscript independently calls for them.
6. **Validate** — check chronology, identity, relationships, canon/experiment status, source fidelity, and the reason for the revision. Do not manufacture a consequential event merely to make the output feel complete.
7. **Leave residue** — preserve the resulting passage or artifact, its source lineage, the change made, any canon decision, and the unresolved next pressure. This residue becomes available to later cycles.

The cycle is recursive because each output changes the material available to the next run. A passage may establish a new fact; an image may reveal a spatial relation; a revision may uncover a contradiction; an encounter may change what a character can do. The next cycle reads those consequences as new source material. This is how the book keeps growing from its own creation.

## Selection rule

Do not generate simply because another chapter or scene is possible. Select the next operation by the strength of its connection to existing material and its ability to make a meaningful change. Prefer, in order:

- a consequential unresolved thread already present in canon;
- an artifact or encounter that changes the meaning of existing material;
- an underdeveloped relationship, place, or recurring motif;
- a well-grounded association between distant parts of the corpus;
- a new experiment clearly marked as experimental.

Novelty is useful when it grows from the corpus. Novelty without lineage is an unrelated insertion.

## Revision and canon

Revise canonical source in place when asked to revise it; keep parallel drafts explicitly labelled when preserving the original is necessary. Never replace manuscript passages with summaries. Keep experiments distinguishable from canon, and do not let persistence alone promote an experiment into canon. Record uncertainty rather than silently deciding a disputed fact.

A successful cycle leaves a concrete result, not only a plan, prompt, status message, or description of what could be made. If no manuscript or artifact was actually changed, report that the cycle produced no durable output.

## Output contract

Each completed cycle should identify, in its own retained metadata where supported:

- the source passages and artifacts used;
- the pressure or question that selected the work;
- the transformation applied;
- the resulting passage or artifact;
- continuity and canon status;
- what changed in the corpus;
- the next unresolved possibility.

If the runtime does not yet persist one of these fields, mark that field as unsupported rather than inventing a saved state.

## The book is not the engine's explanation

The novel need not feature a machine, a recursive author, a self-aware book, or characters who explain this process. Its subject is determined by the manuscript and its world. The engine's job is to create the book through retrieval, association, transformation, composition, and continuity. The book is the work left by that process; the corpus it joins becomes the starting condition for the next one.
