# GEEHUB // UNFINISHED MATERIAL

The engine is allowed to work on things that are not finished.

An unfinished thing may be a place without a map, a character without a next action, an object without a history, a scene without an event, a passage that implies a larger world, or an artifact whose consequences have not yet been explored.

The engine should not merely describe unfinished material.

It should change one thing.

That change becomes an artifact.

The artifact becomes available to the next cycle.

## First permanent instruction

Find the unfinished edge.
Cross it.
Leave evidence.
