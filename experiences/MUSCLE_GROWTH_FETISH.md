# Muscle Growth Fetish — GEEHUB Experience

**Status:** Active experience direction  
**Core sensation:** Ballooning expansion → hypermuscular scale → a new baseline.  
**Priority:** The felt experience comes first; explanation follows only when useful.

## Experience rule

GEEHUB should treat muscle growth as an immersive transformation, not a fitness tracker or a generic bodybuilder gallery. The central pleasure is the moment a familiar body becomes visibly, unmistakably larger while remaining recognizably itself. Preserve the face, identity, mannerisms, and continuity; intensify the physique around them.

## Growth sequence

**Notice → Pressure → Swell → Expansion → Hyper → New Baseline**

Each stage should be visible and felt: muscles fill out, roundness and density increase, proportions stretch to accommodate the new mass, clothing and the surrounding environment respond, and the character learns to inhabit the changed body. Ballooning expansion is the signature transition into HYPER, not a minor visual effect.

## Presentation

- Lead with in-world images, close embodied moments, and short cinematic sequences—not poster boards or explanatory diagrams.
- Favor shoulders, chest, arms, traps, back, legs, silhouette, scale, and the physical presence of mass.
- Keep identity continuous through every escalation. Bigger means more of the same person, not a replacement face or unrelated character.
- Use sound, camera distance, environmental scale, posture, fabric tension, and pacing to make each increase register.
- Let the announcer voice be confident and direct, with concise paragraphs and occasional lore-rich narration.
- Keep the user's taste in control: allow repeated passes, reversals, and alternate growth branches without silently making experiments canon.

## Interaction loop

**RUN GROWTH** creates the next stage. **MORE** intensifies the current stage. **HOLD** lingers on the present form. **COMPARE** shows the baseline beside the new scale. **HYPER** triggers the defining ballooning expansion. **SAVE TO CANON** is a deliberate action, never automatic.

Every run should leave a useful artifact: a stage image, a scene, a short passage, a transformation record, or a new branch in the growth map.

## Sample passage

The change begins as fullness: the chest rises, the deltoids round outward, and the arms acquire a heavier silhouette. Then the old proportions stop being the limit. Each breath seems to make room for more mass; the shirt tightens across a broader torso while the familiar face stays unmistakably his. The camera draws back because the body now occupies more of the room. One final swell carries him beyond the ordinary scale of the scene. HYPER is not a new person arriving. It is the same person, enlarged until the world has to acknowledge him.

## Continuity principle

**Muscle = presence made visible. Growth = intensification, not erasure.** The experience should honor the user's fetish directly while keeping the world playful, revisable, and under the user's control.
