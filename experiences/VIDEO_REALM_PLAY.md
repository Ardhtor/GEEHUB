# VIDEO REALM // MEN IN THE REALM

A second temporal mode for GEEHUB's perceptual layer.

Three adult male figures enter one shared electronic space. The scene is driven by movement and mutual attention rather than narration: arrival, noticing, play, crossing, togetherness, rest.

The video is generated in-browser with Canvas + MediaRecorder and can be exported as WebM.

The figures are reusable temporal actors. Future versions can replace their visual state with canonical GEEHUB characters, imported assets, or generated states.
