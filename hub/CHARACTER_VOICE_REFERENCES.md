## Review state

These are user-designated source mappings. The current public web viewer could not open the five profile pages, and indexed search did not supply a representative collection of their posts. Accordingly, the repository does not claim to have established account-specific personality findings yet. The current voice notes in each character record are derived from pre-existing GEEHUB canon, not invented observations of these account holders.

The machine-readable map is `hub/character-personality-references.json`. The profiles are also listed in `hub/social-cyberspace.json` and appear in the common feed's source selector. Sharing an individual post will bring it into the feed for a character-specific response.

## How the references should refine characters

A later review should examine public posts and captions rather than infer a personality from a username, profile image, or isolated post. Record source URLs and visible dates. Look for recurring public-facing patterns: sentence length and cadence, humour, conversational directness, recurring topics, how the account responds to other people, and how it expresses approval, disagreement, surprise, or enthusiasm. Separate direct observations from the interpretation used to shape the fictional character.

Use broad stylistic qualities rather than copying exact phrases, catchphrases, or distinctive creative expression. The character should remain recognizably part of GEEHUB. The account can refine the delivery, not silently replace the character's history, motives, relationships, or interior life. Do not infer private psychology, health, relationships, or other sensitive details about the account holders.

## Existing character foundations

- **Luke:** direct, protective, body-aware, social; his attention begins with the person and the room around him.
- **Tyler:** curious, skeptical, precise, quietly competitive; he looks for measurable change and what no longer matches.
- **Chase:** observant, decisive, dry, narrative-minded; he waits for the consequence and chooses what matters.
- **Gabe:** process-aware, identity-conscious, growth-driven; he tracks how attention and social signal alter the next baseline.
- **Andrew:** playful, active, improvisational, social; he turns an opening into a concrete action or exchange.

These foundations predate the account mapping and remain active while the external references await direct review.
