# GEEHUB Constellation

GEEHUB is the umbrella for a set of related projects rather than a single application.

## Core creative systems

**BEEFYTHIQ** — generative grammar for accumulation, escalation, stress, mutation, and new baselines.

**The Complex** — continuity layer connecting worlds, artifacts, experiments, and creations.

**Growth Canvas** — visual/rule-based transformation workspace.

## Visual production

**Blender Stand / 3D Workspace** — modeling, sculpting, posing, scene building, and rendering.

**Growth Tools** — image/video tooling and subject-aware experiments.

**Retro Lab** — visual and sonic experiments drawing on late-90s/2000s and Y2K-era interfaces.

## Fiction and myth

**Muscle Myth** — pantheon, gods, artifacts, and mythic characters.

**The Facility** — clinical research-complex setting for fictional experiments and encounters.

**Liminal Gains** — game-oriented growth/transformation prototype.

**BIG BRUTEFORCE** — compact mutation-engine game prototype.

## Research and discovery

**BodyLounger Research Archive** — historical link/provenance archive.

**Discovery Engine** — personalized discovery and recommendation experiments.

**Religion as Dialogue** — long-form research and thesis workspace.

## Expansion rule

A project does not need to be complete before it gets a place in the constellation. It needs a distinct identity, a useful boundary, and enough continuity that future work can attach to it.

When a node becomes substantial, give it its own repository. The atlas should keep the relationship visible rather than absorbing the node back into one giant codebase.
