# GEEHUB Narrative Jammer

This is the easiest door into the constellation.

Open `index.html`. Press **SURPRISE ME** when you do not know what to work on. Press **FOCUS: BODY** when you want a concrete visual anchor. When a collision feels alive, press **MAKE IT** and the jammer turns the thought into a tiny artifact brief.

The design intentionally borrows lessons already present in the wider repository family. The older Stable Diffusion work demonstrates the value of immediate controls, visible variation, saved generation parameters, and the ability to move from one result to another without rebuilding the whole context. The writing/research work emphasizes practice as recursive: act, examine the action, examine the examination, and let the resulting conclusions feed the next action. GEEHUB combines those instincts into a small creative loop.

## The loop

**SURPRISE → SEE → MAKE → SAVE → RETURN**

The first output should be vivid enough to picture. The second should be small enough to make. The saved result should retain its ingredients and the reason it was generated. The return step should make it cheap to try again.

This is deliberately smaller than a full generative platform. It is a doorway, a laboratory bench, and a memory device. Larger systems can grow behind it.

## Imaginal resolution

A jam is judged partly by whether it makes an internal scene easier to hold. Prefer spatial relationships over abstract adjectives. Prefer a camera angle, material, sound, motion, scale, or sequence over a generic instruction to “be creative.” The user should be able to close their eyes briefly and still have something concrete to inspect.

## Provenance

Every useful collision should remain attributable to the projects that produced it. The constellation is the raw material; the jam is a temporary branch; the artifact is the first durable consequence.

## Why the repository keeps the story

The important commentary around the build is part of the implementation. When a new mechanism appears, its README/spec/code comments should explain the pressure that produced it, the failure it addresses, and what new action it makes possible. This preserves enough narrative that the project can be resumed without reconstructing the entire conversation.

## Future directions

A later version can promote saved jams into real issue templates, create project briefs automatically, hand visual briefs to Blender workflows, and connect semantic growth to a real Beefythiq graph. Those additions should remain downstream of the tiny loop above. The front door should stay simple.
