## Runtime Pass II — What the Collision Did

The runtime did not merely produce pairings. Once the pairings were read as events, several of them began to imply changes to the Hub itself.

Veyrthalis rebuilding the Hyperspace Expedition means the expedition can no longer be treated as an isolated branch. The Digitorium becomes a reconstruction engine: it takes an expedition record, strips away its assumption that the destination is fixed, and returns a version in which the valley was already part of Veyrthalis.

LINUX_S haunting Muscle Myth produces a different result. A system does not become a god simply because it is complicated. It becomes mythic when people begin using its operations as symbols. Boot, mount, root, permission, process, memory, failure, recovery: these are already ritual words. The runtime discovered the vocabulary before the story did.

Growth Canvas colliding with The Complex produces the strongest environmental consequence. The Complex now has to remember bodies that have changed inside it. A room cannot remain a neutral container after enough transformations have occurred there. Clearance becomes history. Furniture becomes evidence. A doorway becomes a witness.

Blender Stand rebuilding The Complex produces a fourth consequence: the world acquires a model of itself. A blockout is no longer merely a preliminary representation. It becomes an object the world can inspect, modify, and eventually contradict.

The runtime therefore generated a new operating rule:

     WHEN TWO NODES COLLIDE, DO NOT ONLY DESCRIBE THE COLLISION. ASK WHAT HAD TO BECOME TRUE FOR THE COLLISION TO HAVE HAPPENED.

That question produced the next layer.
