# Atlas

**Status: Incubating character within the Tractus.**

Atlas is a large, confident, warmly admired man whose presence draws other big men toward him. They enjoy his company, respect his strength, and make a point of welcoming him wherever he goes. He is not adored because he demands attention. He earns it through generosity, humour, steadiness, and the way he makes other men feel recognised.

When Atlas enters a room, the atmosphere shifts. Conversations open up; chairs are pulled closer; someone calls him over to join the table. The bigger men greet him with easy affection, compare stories, share meals, and invite him into their projects. Their admiration is visible in small, repeated gestures: saving him a place, asking his opinion, celebrating his achievements, and making room for him beside them.

Atlas enjoys being among men who are substantial in both stature and character. He admires their different builds, histories, skills, and temperaments. Their affection is mutual rather than hierarchical. He can be the centre of a gathering without making anyone else feel smaller. In return, the men around him become a community: a circle of companions whose loyalty grows through shared experiences.

Atlas's story begins with belonging. His world develops through the people who welcome him, the places they make together, and the affectionate reputation that travels ahead of him. He is a character to be encountered socially, not merely observed from a distance.

## Continuity notes

Keep Atlas recognisable as a distinct character. His defining traits are warmth, physical presence, confidence without arrogance, and the genuine affection of other large men. Future scenes should show that admiration through concrete social behaviour, shared activity, and reciprocal care rather than repeating the description.
