# LUKE // INFINITE GROWTH

Status: canon / active engine law.

Infinite growth is not a final oversized form. It is the point where growth becomes the rule by which the world organizes itself.

## Operating law

**INHERIT → GROW → CATCH UP → EXPAND THE WORLD → LIVE AT THE NEW SCALE → ARCHIVE → INHERIT AGAIN**

Every transformation starts from Luke's actual retained baseline. Height, limb length, shoulders, chest, arms, and legs develop together so mass remains embodied rather than becoming a disconnected exaggeration. The previous maximum is never discarded; it becomes the floor of the next phase.

## What remains invariant

Luke remains Luke. Preserve his recognizable face, name, manner, personality, relationships, and accumulated history. Scale does not erase personhood. He can still walk around, talk, joke, train, rest, and go about ordinary business.

## What changes

The environment becomes responsive. At first the gym's framing and equipment make the new size legible. Then the room, ceiling, floor, architecture, light placement, camera distance, horizon, and spatial sound adjust. Each environment catches up only to become the next measuring instrument.

## Infinite does not mean visually static

Every new pass must create a perceptible consequence: a proportional body change, a shift in movement or stance, a changed relation to the room, a new spatial scale, or an ordinary activity made newly possible. Avoid repeating the same scale jump without a consequence. Let a milestone settle long enough to be witnessed, then continue.

## The limit

There is no largest Luke and no terminal scene. At the limit, the distinction between Luke growing and the world becoming capable of containing him loses meaning. This is an open-ended process, not a claim that a completed infinite state has been reached.

## Implementation contract

- No terminal growth cap.
- Persist body state, stage, witness count, and history between advances.
- Preserve proportional stature and limb length.
- Couple growth to environment response.
- Archive milestones and compact deltas rather than millions of duplicate records.
- Keep reset as an explicit user action only; ordinary growth never resets continuity.

**Engine line:** Every settled scale is a home for a moment, and the beginning of the next scale.
