# NOTHING PERSONAL — Monument Sign

**Status:** digital design / proposed Confluence landmark  
**Asset:** [NOTHING_PERSONAL_SIGN.svg](../assets/signs/NOTHING_PERSONAL_SIGN.svg)  
**Placement:** the approach to the Exchange Hall, at the edge of the redwood campus plaza  
**Design intent:** monumental, permanent, quietly imposing; a civic threshold rather than a commercial advertisement

The sign is conceived as a freestanding timber portal large enough to register across the plaza. Its structure uses deep cedar and sequoia members, visible grain, dark steel shoes and brackets, and a recessed blue-green illuminated face. The lettering is deliberately blunt: two centered lines, “NOTHING” above “PERSONAL,” in a heavy geometric sans serif. The illumination is cool and restrained, closer to an old institutional display or CRT phosphor than to a flashy neon attraction.

The portal stands across the main pedestrian approach to the Exchange Hall. Its posts are set beyond the clear walking route, with a broad paved apron beneath it so groups, carts, and service traffic can pass without bottlenecking. Redwood trunks frame the view; the Exchange Hall remains visible beyond the sign. At night the letters provide the principal light, while low-level path lighting keeps the ground and the sign's supports legible.

The phrase is intentionally open-ended. It can be read as a warning against taking institutional decisions as personal attacks, as a dry joke about the enormous machinery of the Confluence, or simply as a statement whose meaning belongs to the person arriving. The landmark does not explain itself with a slogan or plaque. Its scale, material, and placement do the work.

**Production notes:** the SVG is a scalable first digital concept, not a measured construction drawing. Dimensions, foundations, structural calculations, lighting levels, accessibility clearances, and final site survey remain to be developed before any physical build.
