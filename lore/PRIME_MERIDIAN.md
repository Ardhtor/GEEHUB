# GEEHUB Prime Meridian

Status: foundational design principle.

The Prime Meridian is GEEHUB's first shared coordinate system: the origin from which its worlds, interfaces, bodies, stories, and engines establish their bearings. It is not merely a visual theme. It is the relationship between form and function made visible.

## Origin rule

Every new system must be able to answer three questions:
- **Where is it oriented?** Establish its relation to the shared world and its current state.
- **What changes its form?** Let the underlying mechanism produce the visual consequence.
- **What persists?** Carry identity, history, and inherited state forward rather than resetting the world to a blank surface.

## The first axis

The Lineage Pantheon is the first visible meridian. Its cold electric light, deep architectural space, encounter stage, and marked origin establish a recognizable bearing. This is a seed, not a universal template: other regions may develop distinct materials, atmospheres, and rhythms while retaining continuity with the origin.

## Functional coordinates

- **Continuity / Gee:** what stays available and remembers.
- **Identity / Luke:** what remains recognizable through transformation.
- **Scale / Swolebeasts:** how mass, height, proportion, and presence alter the scene.
- **Inertia / Hulum's Cave:** how motion, timing, camera, light, and sound leave traces in later states.
- **Care / Daddy:** how power makes room for others.
- **World order / the gods:** how larger forces impose depth, rules, and consequence.

These are working coordinates, not decorative labels. An interaction should change the world through one or more of them, and the resulting state should be available to the next interaction.

## Form-as-function contract

1. Show the current state as a place or experience, not only as a status report.
2. Make controls act on the world they describe.
3. Make consequential changes visible.
4. Preserve lineage and event history.
5. Let visual form arise from the mechanism; do not let styling pretend that an unimplemented mechanism exists.
6. Keep the origin recognizable while allowing every branch to grow its own local character.

The durable rule: **GEEHUB does not merely draw a world. It gives change a place to occur, and gives that place a memory.**
