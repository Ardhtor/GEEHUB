# Tractus Particles

Small, independently readable pieces of continuity. Each particle carries one image, action, object, or change. They are deliberately brief: a reader can enter anywhere, while repeated names and objects allow the pieces to gather into a larger history.

## Northgate

**N-01 — The leather loop.** The spare key no longer hangs by its weakened ring. Tomas tied a leather thong through the bow. Neral checks the knot whenever he closes the gate.

**N-02 — Two signatures.** The register has room for both the man handing over the key and the man receiving it. The empty line is now more noticeable than the ink.

**N-03 — Rain on the page.** A damp crescent remains beside the late-cart entry. No one has recopied it. The page is legible, and the mark belongs to that night.

## Hrothgar Hall

**H-01 — The warm bowl.** Rovan arrives after the stew has been served. The cook covers a bowl and leaves it beside the hearth. Nobody asks him to explain the delay before he eats.

**H-02 — The hard crust.** The loaf is overbaked on one side. Three men prefer that end. The cook says nothing, but begins turning the tray halfway through the next bake.

**H-03 — The empty place.** The card returns to the cabinet after supper. A faint ring of broth marks one corner. The steward records its return and leaves the stain alone.

**H-04 — The chair leg.** One chair rocks on the stone floor. Brograntimax folds a scrap of leather beneath the short leg. The chair stops moving. The scrap is added to the repair list, not the furniture inventory.

## The Laundry

**L-01 — Token eighteen.** Arlen's initials remain on the token. They describe an earlier inscription, not ownership. The token continues to move between residents.

**L-02 — Blue thread.** Mikel keeps a short length of thread tied around the basket handle. It is long enough for one repair and too short to become clutter.

**L-03 — The missing peg.** A numbered peg turns up in a shirt pocket. Its owner returns it without being asked. The laundry ledger gains one sentence and no reprimand.

## Moon Temple

**M-01 — Eleven minutes.** The third bell sounds eleven minutes late. The delay is written beside the safety amendment, not over the original time.

**M-02 — The reflective plate.** Aven angles the plate until the lantern can be seen from the upper landing. He checks it again after dusk.

**M-03 — Linen in the box.** The oil-marked cloth is folded beneath the spare wick. It is kept because it worked, not because it is old.

## Observation Ring

**O-01 — The sharpened pencil.** Orven leaves the blue pencil beside the card tray. Its point is ready for the next observer.

**O-02 — A thumb counting.** The trainee records the movement without deciding what it means. The man at the screen waits for the pattern to return.

**O-03 — No card today.** Orven watches the dark screen and makes no entry. He is learning that attention does not always need to become a record.

## The Market and the Road

**R-01 — The curled corner.** Rovan's bridge report dries beside the stove. One corner stays curled. He keeps it that way so he can find the warning quickly.

**R-02 — Two kinds of map.** Dan's pocket map describes the road. Brograntimax's work order describes the cart. They are stored together but never substituted for one another.

**R-03 — Dried pears.** Samir adds a small sack to the next Hrothgar delivery. The kitchen had not requested it. The cook uses them in the morning porridge.

**R-04 — The upper turn.** The cart reaches the storehouse gate at an angle. Dan stands outside and signals the driver. The wheel clears the stone by less than a hand's width.

## The Cabin

**C-01 — The dry drawer.** The upper-room letter remains folded. Privathor checks the drawer after rain and does not touch the seal.

**C-02 — Cedar dust.** A little cedar dust remains where the strip was fitted beneath the desk rail. Lexor sweeps it into his palm and tips it into the stove.

**C-03 — The unanswered space.** The inventory records the letter's location, condition, and access category. It records no guess about its contents.

## Repair Yard

**Y-01 — The cord's memory.** The measuring cord carries a small dark mark where the old cart's axle was measured. Brograntimax does not cut that section away.

**Y-02 — The wheel guard.** The new guard is narrower. The first test is made with an empty cart; the loaded test remains open.

**Y-03 — A borrowed gauge.** The brass gauge returns to its hook with a note from Northgate. Its next inspection is due before the wet season.

## Loose Particles

**X-01 — A dry pair of boots.** Someone leaves a pair of boots beside the hall stove. In the morning they are dry, and a second pair has been placed beside them.

**X-02 — The unclaimed button.** The button remains separate from the folded cloth. The first mistaken association is preserved in the cabinet note, but no longer treated as fact.

**X-03 — A name spoken correctly.** A new porter mispronounces Neral's name. Tomas corrects him once, quietly. The porter repeats it correctly at the next shift.

**X-04 — The door held open.** A man carrying two sacks reaches the hall entrance. The door is already being held. Neither man makes a point of the exchange.

**X-05 — The last lamp.** The kitchen lamp is extinguished after the room has emptied. The steward notes the oil remaining in the reservoir. Tomorrow's entry begins with the same measure.

## Particle Rules

A particle should be small enough to read in one breath and concrete enough to remember. It may contain a person, object, gesture, sound, place, or changed condition. It should not explain the whole world around it.

Particles can stand alone, but they should not be isolated from continuity. When a particle repeats an object or person, it should respect the established state. When it changes something, the change should be traceable. When it introduces uncertainty, it should leave that uncertainty open rather than quietly resolving it.

A particle may later grow into a scene, a letter, a chapter, a map annotation, a voice, or an interactive object. Expansion should preserve the original small record as an earlier state. The short form is not a rough draft to be discarded. It is a compact point of entry into the larger world.

The Fog Week is one sequence. These particles are many entrances into it.
