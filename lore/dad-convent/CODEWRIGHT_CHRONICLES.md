# THE GRAMMATON

Active Dad Convent narrative system. The Grammaton is the first codewright: he turns measurements, conditions, remembered decisions, and shared intentions into instructions that other residents can understand, test, revise, and inherit.

## Living grammar

DECLARE names a thing. BUILD proposes a structure. ALTER revises a state without erasing its history. INHERIT passes a creation and its provenance to a successor. TRANSMIT sends an artifact to another narrative with its origin and intended recipient intact.

The Architect writes boundaries and load paths. The Builder writes sequences of practical work. The Keeper records provenance. The Witness observes how spaces are actually used. The Heir forks prior designs while preserving their ancestry.

## First Correspondence: DC-VEYR-001

The Grammaton sends a timber-joint plan from the Dad Convent to the Keeper in Veyrthalis. The Keeper adapts it for damp stone and fog, adding drainage clearance and a replaceable bearing surface. The returned drawing changes the Great Hall's eastern gallery and gives the Veyrthalis archive a new construction method.

The Grammaton's note: *Do not ask the stone to remain dry. Give the water somewhere to go.*

The Witness sees the test joint and proposes applying the same principle to the orchard paths. One exchange has generated a second project.

## Correspondence rules

- Narratives exchange plans, terms, songs, measurements, witness accounts, objects, questions, and code fragments.
- Every artifact retains sender, source narrative, destination, version, context, and reply chain.
- Transmission is not a command. The receiving narrative interprets and decides whether to accept, adapt, or decline.
- Forks preserve parent versions. Canon and experiment remain distinguishable.
- Characters may misunderstand an artifact; the misunderstanding remains part of the record rather than being rewritten as the sender's intent.
- Fictional code does not silently execute against real GEEHUB files. External execution requires an implemented, verified bridge.
