# Joey Prever — The Colossus Chronicles

**GEEHUB story library · adult male bodybuilding fantasy · six-volume cycle · LOVE continuity**

The Colossus Chronicles is a serialized, character-led muscle-growth saga. Its subject is not simply a man becoming larger: it is the way extraordinary physical development changes training, clothing, architecture, friendship, self-image, and the social life of a residential bodybuilding community. Joey is the central continuing character. The story treats him as an adult fictionalized character and preserves his recognizable temperament, humour, affection, and relationships while allowing the fictional transformation to become fantastical.

The series uses an all-male cast of adult fictional characters. They are not interchangeable physiques. Each man has his own voice, training history, ambitions, habits, humour, and relationship to the community. The phrase “white bros” is treated as a casting/aesthetic note for this particular fictional ensemble, never as a ranking of people or a claim about real-world groups.

The volumes are written as actual fiction: continuous scenes, dialogue, training sessions, detailed but non-explicit physical description, measurements as story artifacts, posing and presentation, clothing and equipment, character interactions, and consequences that persist. This is adult-oriented bodybuilding fantasy, not explicit sexual content.

## Canon rules

- Joey's Stage 5 is **COLOSSUS / LOVE ASCENDANT**. It is already colossal; later stages begin from that scale rather than resetting him to an ordinary bodybuilder.
- Growth is full-body and balanced in narrative emphasis: deltoids, chest, back, arms, forearms, trunk, glutes, thighs, and calves all receive attention. No volume relies on a single repeated adjective.
- Exact measurements are only canon when recorded in the continuity ledger. New numbers introduced in a scene must be added there or clearly marked as an estimate.
- Growth is fantastical and internally consistent, not presented as real-world physiology or training advice.
- The environment responds: tailored clothing, reinforced furniture, expanded changing rooms, custom equipment, revised transport and circulation, and new social rituals.
- LOVE is not a prize or a temporary buff. It is the durable bond that lets Joey remain himself as everything around him changes.
- Other characters have agency. They can admire, tease, disagree, compete, collaborate, and pursue their own arcs.
- The story does not claim knowledge of any real person's private thoughts or feelings. Joey in this series is a fictional character.

The residence is a beginning, not the boundary of the series. [WORLD_ATLAS_BEYOND_THE_COMMONS.md](WORLD_ATLAS_BEYOND_THE_COMMONS.md) maps the Meridian Basin around the campus: Crownmere, the River Aven, the Long Park, Glassmarket, Foundry Ward, Old Crown, Observatory Rise, Cedar Reach, the Eastern Tablelands, Saltline Coast, and the Northern Ranges. Volume VI begins that outward movement in [Chapter 4 — Sunday Beyond the Gate](VOLUME_VI_THE_SCALE_COMMONS.md). The city has its own institutions, workers, clubs, routines, and unresolved problems; the residents become part of its life rather than the centre of every event.

The larger Tractus atlas is [hub/WORLD_ATLAS.md](../../hub/WORLD_ATLAS.md). It keeps the Colossus Chronicles, Veyrthalis, Mexiancas, the Sacred Corridor, and the other worlds distinct while recording the ways artifacts and encounters can pass between them.

## Reading order and continuation

Read the volumes in sequence. New chapters should be appended to the relevant volume and summarized in the continuity ledger. Do not overwrite earlier stages to make later ones seem more impressive. Preserve the history of each baseline.

## Current state

Volume VI opens with the Scale Commons becoming a permanent part of the Colossus campus. Joey's established Stage 5 Colossus baseline and subsequent beyond-baseline growth remain intact; the new scale classes describe measured height for environmental planning and do not replace his personal growth stages. Marcus enters the Commons at a recorded eight feet three inches. LOVE remains the series' continuity principle.
