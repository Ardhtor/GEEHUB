# Volume III — Colossus

*The Colossus Chronicles · Book Three · Stage 5*

## Chapter 1 — The New Baseline

By the time the new season began, Joey's former measurements belonged to another version of the story. Stage 5 had arrived so decisively that the residents stopped comparing him with ordinary bodybuilding reference points. He had become colossal: an immense, fantastical mass-titan whose development was visible across his entire frame. His shoulders formed enormous rounded caps; his chest carried extraordinary depth and breadth; his back had become a vast structure of width and thickness. His upper arms and forearms were massive, his waist and trunk had developed to support the scale of his torso, and his thighs and calves had grown into powerful pillars beneath him. There was no isolated trick to the transformation. The whole physique had advanced.

The first morning he returned to the Mirror Room, the residents were already there. Martin had arranged the new camera at a lower angle, Daniel had marked the floor with fresh measurement points, and Ellis had brought the revised assessment sheets. Joey stepped into the room wearing a simple dark training shirt and custom-cut shorts. The clothing had been made for his current proportions, but even it looked temporary against the scale of the changes.

Noah stared for a moment, then looked at the old measurement board. “We need a new board.”

“We need a new room,” Daniel replied.

Joey laughed. The sound loosened the atmosphere. He was aware of their attention, but it did not feel like being examined by strangers. These were the men who had known him before the first entry in the training book, before the Mirror Room, before the equipment order. They had watched each stage arrive. Their astonishment was part of the history, not a substitute for it.

## Chapter 2 — The Colossus Facility

The gym could no longer remain a collection of rooms that happened to contain equipment. It became a facility with departments. The central training hall received reinforced platforms and modular stations. The Mirror Room expanded into a presentation suite with adjustable camera positions and a gallery for reviewing movement. The changing rooms gained wider circulation and custom storage. A tailoring room was established beside the equipment workshop so that clothing and training accessories could be adapted without sending every request outside the residence.

The dormitory changed as well. Joey's room was moved to a larger suite near the central courtyard, not as a throne or a reward but because the old arrangement no longer worked. Furniture was commissioned to suit the new scale. The corridor was redesigned to make movement easier for everyone, and the common room gained larger seating areas. The changes benefited the residents collectively. A place that had once been a modest training residence was becoming a purpose-built campus.

Joey took part in the planning meetings. He was careful not to let every decision become about him. “If we're rebuilding the gym,” he told Daniel, “make it useful for the next guy too.”

Daniel nodded. “That's the point. Build for the future, not just the current record.”

## Chapter 3 — The New Kind of Attention

The Colossus baseline changed the way people noticed Joey. Visitors sometimes fell quiet when he entered the hall. Residents from other buildings came to see the new equipment and stayed to talk. The attention could be flattering, but it could also become tiring. Joey learned to distinguish admiration from familiarity. A stranger might be impressed by his size; the men who knew him understood that he still preferred a quiet breakfast, disliked being interrupted halfway through a conversation, and laughed hardest at jokes that were not particularly clever.

One evening, after the last training session, Joey sat with Martin in the courtyard. The new furniture had arrived that afternoon. The evening light caught the pale stone walls, and somewhere inside the building Noah was arguing with Daniel about the correct way to label the pantry shelves.

“You've changed the whole place,” Martin said.

“We changed it,” Joey answered.

Martin considered that, then smiled. “Fair.”

The volume ends with the Colossus facility opening officially. The residents gather for a photograph beneath the new sign. Joey stands in the centre, enormous but relaxed, surrounded by the men who helped make the place. The sign reads: **COLOSSUS — A PLACE TO KEEP BECOMING**.

Stage 5 is not the end of Joey's growth. It is the first baseline that the entire institution has been built to support.
