# Volume IV — Love Ascendant

*The Colossus Chronicles · Book Four*

## Chapter 1 — What Remains Familiar

The new suite was quiet in the early morning. Beyond the windows, the campus was beginning to wake: a delivery cart crossed the courtyard, lights came on in the dining hall, and the first residents made their way toward the training floor. Joey stood by the wardrobe, looking at the custom clothing Peter had delivered the previous evening. Each piece had been made for the body he now inhabited, with room for further change built into the seams. He chose a simple outfit and set it aside.

The scale of his physique had become ordinary to him in one sense and extraordinary in another. He could feel its presence in every practical decision: how he turned through a doorway, how he settled into a chair, how much space he needed at the table. Yet the details that made him Joey had not disappeared. He still smiled before making a joke. He still remembered what people had told him. He still preferred company that did not demand a performance.

LOVE entered the story here not as a dramatic revelation but as a pattern of attention. It was the accumulated knowledge of another person: the ability to recognize when Joey wanted company and when he wanted quiet, to notice the small changes that a crowd overlooked, and to remain interested in the man rather than the spectacle. The relationship did not make his growth happen by magic. It gave the transformation a human centre.

## Chapter 2 — The Ordinary Ritual

The residents began a weekly supper in the common room. No speeches, no scoring, no official photographs unless someone asked. They cooked, brought food, argued about music, and traded stories from the week. Joey often arrived later than the others because his training sessions had become longer and the facility's schedule had to accommodate his work. The group kept a place for him without making an announcement of it.

One night, Noah had prepared far too much food and insisted that this was a deliberate strategy. Daniel arrived with a repaired lamp. Ellis brought a stack of photographs from the Mirror Room, including one in which Martin had managed to look serious for almost half a second. Joey laughed so hard that he had to put his glass down.

It was a small evening, but it mattered. The Colossus facility could expand, the training systems could be redesigned, and Joey's body could continue toward scales that had once seemed impossible. None of those developments could replace the ordinary rituals that made the place feel inhabited. The supper became a fixed point in the calendar. When residents were away, they sent messages. When someone had a difficult week, the others made sure he was not left to manage it alone.

## Chapter 3 — Love Ascendant

Joey's next transformation began gradually and then became impossible to ignore. His shoulders expanded beyond the Stage 5 outline, his chest gained another immense layer of depth, and his back widened until the old camera framing could no longer contain him comfortably. His arms grew heavier and fuller; his legs developed to carry the new mass. The tailoring room revised his patterns. The facility commissioned new supports and expanded the presentation suite.

The change was spectacular, but the scene that defined the stage took place after the measurements were finished. Joey stood before the mirror while the others packed away the equipment. He looked at the new record, then at the old photograph from the first volume. The distance between those two images was almost unbelievable.

Martin came to stand beside him. “Still you,” he said.

Joey nodded. “Still me.”

That was the point. LOVE ASCENDANT meant that growth could become immense without erasing continuity. Joey did not have to choose between becoming extraordinary and remaining recognizable. The people who cared about him were not attached to one particular size, one photograph, or one stage. They were attached to the continuing person.

The volume closes with the weekly supper underway. Joey arrives late, as usual. Noah has saved him a place. The conversation moves around him and includes him without stopping for a grand entrance. Outside, the Colossus facility stands lit against the evening sky. Inside, the ordinary ritual continues.
