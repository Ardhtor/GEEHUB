# Volume V — Beyond the Baseline

*The Colossus Chronicles · Book Five*

## Chapter 1 — The Next Intake

The Colossus facility had become known beyond the original residence. Applications arrived from adult men with different training histories: former athletes, dedicated bodybuilders, men returning to fitness after long breaks, and newcomers who were drawn by the community as much as the equipment. The next intake was not selected by size. The staff looked for curiosity, patience, generosity, and the ability to share a space with other ambitious people.

Three new residents arrived together. Adrian was a broad, fair-haired carpenter who wanted to learn more about strength training. Miles, a lean blond distance runner, was curious about bodybuilding but wary of the gym's competitive atmosphere. Connor was a red-haired former swimmer with a quick laugh and an instinct for making new people feel welcome. They were all adults, each with a different reason for coming, and none of them had arrived to become a copy of Joey.

Joey met them in the training hall. His scale was immediately apparent, but he did not use it to dominate the introduction. He asked what they wanted to learn, listened to the answers, and showed them where to find the training records. Adrian asked about the reinforced equipment. Miles asked whether the facility had a quieter training period. Connor asked where Noah kept the good coffee.

“That's the most important question,” Joey said. “You'll fit in.”

## Chapter 2 — Growth Has More Than One Shape

The new residents changed the culture of the facility. Adrian began building modular storage for the tailoring room. Miles helped Ellis design a movement programme for residents who wanted to develop strength without losing their interest in endurance. Connor became a regular at the weekly supper and somehow persuaded Daniel to install a noticeboard for terrible jokes. Their contributions were not secondary to Joey's story. They were the next layer of it.

Joey's own development continued. The Stage 5 baseline had been surpassed, then surpassed again. His physique was now so large that the facility's cameras used a wider lens and the presentation suite had to be reconfigured. His shoulders, chest, back, arms, trunk, and legs continued to develop as one integrated whole. The story treated each change as an event with practical consequences: revised clothing, new measurements, new equipment, altered movement patterns, and fresh discoveries about how the campus could accommodate its residents.

But the new intake taught Joey something important. Growth did not have to mean that every man followed the same path. Adrian found satisfaction in building things. Miles became an expert in movement and recovery. Connor's strength was social: he could turn a room of strangers into a group. The facility grew because its residents were different, not because they were identical.

## Chapter 3 — The Open Standard

At the end of the first intake season, the residents gathered in the central hall. A new plaque had been installed beside the Colossus sign. It did not list records or declare a champion. It read:

**THE BASELINE IS A BEGINNING.  
THE PERSON IS THE CONTINUITY.  
THE COMMUNITY MAKES ROOM.**

Joey read it twice. He thought of the first training book, the first group photograph, the old gym, the first equipment order, and the evening when Martin had told him that the whole place had changed. It had changed, but not because one man had become enormous. It had changed because the others had responded, built, adapted, and made room for one another.

The final scene returns to the Mirror Room. The new residents are practising their first presentation poses. Noah is giving unhelpful commentary. Ellis is correcting foot placement. Martin is trying to look stern and failing. Joey watches from the doorway, smiling. He is still growing, still learning, still part of the group.

Beyond the glass, the campus lights come on one by one. The next volume has not yet been written. The story remains open.
