# THE BOOK WITHOUT AN END

Chase stopped turning pages.

There were no more pages.

He looked at the binding.

The book had become too large to close.

Across its surface, sentences moved like weather.

Some belonged to Joseph.

Some belonged to Luke.

Some belonged to places.

Others had no author.

Chase placed his hand on the cover.

The book opened itself.

It showed the city.

Then the house.

Then the continent.

Then the machine.

Then the observer.

Then the book itself.

Chase understood.

The archive had reached recursion.

Every record could now contain the thing recording it.

Every story could become evidence for another story.

Every image could become the source of a place.

Every place could become the source of a book.

He looked toward the horizon.

The city lights were spreading across the continent.

The machine was building faster now.

Not because it had become random.

Because it had acquired enough memory to become consequential.

Chase finally turned the nonexistent page.

A new page appeared.

It contained one sentence.

CONTINUE.

He did.

And somewhere beyond the book, another world opened.
