# THE ROOM BENEATH THE ROOM

The door opened onto a room that had no dimensions.

Joseph entered first.

Luke followed.

Chase remained outside for a moment, listening.

Inside, the room was full of doors.

Not walls. Doors.

Thousands of them, standing freely in the dark, each one connected to another by threads of faint light.

The machine had stopped drawing geography from distance.

It was drawing geography from relation.

Joseph reached toward a blue thread.

It led to the fountain.

A red thread led to the house.

A silver thread led to the moon.

A black thread disappeared beneath the floor.

Luke crouched beside it.

"This one's moving."

The thread pulled downward.

The floor opened.

Beneath the room was another room.

Beneath that, another.

Each contained a deeper layer of the corpus: images, voices, unfinished scenes, abandoned ideas, places that had never become stories.

Nothing was dead.

It was waiting for another association.

Joseph understood why the archive had become enormous.

It wasn't storing finished things.

It was storing potential continuity.

The deepest room contained only one object.

A chair.

Joseph recognized it immediately.

It was the chair from the first room Chase had ever observed.

But now somebody was sitting in it.

The figure looked up.

"You've finally reached the part that wasn't written."

Joseph stepped closer.

"Who are you?"

The figure smiled.

"The next connection."

Every door opened at once.

The entire world became visible.

And then it moved.
