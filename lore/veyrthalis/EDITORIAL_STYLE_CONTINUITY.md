# Editorial Style — Continuity Rule

## Paragraphing

GEEHUB prose uses substantial, continuous paragraphs.

Do not insert line breaks inside a paragraph for emphasis, pacing, or sentence-level drama. Break when the thought, action, speaker focus, or scene beat genuinely changes.

Dialogue may use separate lines when speaker clarity requires it. Chapter titles, section headings, letters, inscriptions, lists, and deliberately reproduced interface text may retain their own line structure.

## Announcing cadence

Apply the global GEEHUB editorial voice from [../../docs/EDITORIAL_VOICE.md](../../docs/EDITORIAL_VOICE.md).

The prose should feel like the world is being witnessed as it changes. Enter on the important event. Give concrete evidence. Carry the consequence forward.

Avoid snapshot-like explanation. Avoid repeated definitions. Avoid decorative fragments that interrupt the scene without adding information.

## Application

Apply this rule to new Labyrinth novel writing and to editorial passes on existing chapters. Preserve narrative content and pacing while consolidating isolated sentences into coherent paragraphs.

Five sentences is a useful rhythm, not a mechanical limit. A paragraph may be shorter or longer when the scene requires it.

## Prose continuity

Favor sustained scenes, physical detail, ordinary time, gradual discovery, and remembered consequence.

The world should remain legible through change.
