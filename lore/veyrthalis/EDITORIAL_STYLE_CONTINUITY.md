# Editorial Style — Continuity Rule

## Paragraphing

Default prose formatting uses continuous paragraphs of approximately five sentences. Do not insert line breaks within a paragraph for emphasis, pacing, or sentence-level drama. Break only when the paragraph's thought, action, speaker focus, or scene beat genuinely changes.

Dialogue may use separate lines when needed to keep speakers clear. Chapter titles, section headings, letters, inscriptions, lists, and deliberately reproduced interface text may retain their own line structure.

## Application

Apply this rule to all new Labyrinth novel writing and to editorial passes on existing chapters. Preserve the narrative content and pacing while consolidating isolated sentences into coherent paragraphs. Do not convert every paragraph into exactly five sentences; five is a target rhythm, not a mechanical limit.

## Prose Continuity

Favor sustained scenes, physical detail, ordinary time, and gradual discovery. Avoid snapshot-like sequences and ornamental line breaks. Let tension emerge from what characters notice, do, and fail to understand.
