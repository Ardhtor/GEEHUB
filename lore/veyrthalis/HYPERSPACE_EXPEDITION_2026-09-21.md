# Veyrthalis — Hyperspace Expedition

**Record date:** 2026-09-21  
**Status:** Experimental continuation; not yet promoted to canon

## Expedition state

Four men have descended through a subterranean labyrinth and reached a vast valley containing an immense city and an ancient, living presence. Kirk and Joseph are among the four. The city appears integrated with the presence rather than merely built around it.

The expedition's arrival has triggered a response: lights move through the city in coordinated waves, an illuminated path leads toward a subterranean entrance, and a voice addresses the men. The voice claims they have returned to a place from which they were never entirely absent. Kirk and Joseph experience anomalous, shared impressions, but their ordinary identities and mutual recognition remain intact.

## Scene continuation — The Digitorium

The path took the four men through black trees whose branches formed intricate geometries overhead. At the tunnel entrance, immense figures carved into the walls faced a circular opening. Beyond it lay a chamber of unmeasurable scale, filled with suspended lights arranged like stars, organs, branching nerves, and connected cells.

A translucent surface appeared:

> DIGITORIUM // ACCESS GRANTED

The chamber registered four arrivals, three continuities, and one unresolved transformation. When the men asked what this meant, it answered that the fourth had already begun.

At the chamber's far end, something enormous opened its eyes. The city was only a small portion of its anatomy: towers were embedded in its surface, bridges crossed between its limbs, and districts occupied the folds of its form.

The presence addressed the men, saying they had brought a small world with them. Kirk felt his memories and his bond with Joseph being examined. Joseph called him back to the immediate moment; Kirk answered, "I'm here." The chamber's voice recognized their bond.

The lights overhead arranged themselves into four patterns. Three remained stable. The fourth expanded. Kirk perceived the chamber's architecture as part of a living body, but Joseph's touch and voice restored his immediate sense of scale. Joseph suspected the presence knew why they had come. It replied that they did not yet know themselves.

The chamber went dark. Deep beneath the city, a second heart began to beat.
