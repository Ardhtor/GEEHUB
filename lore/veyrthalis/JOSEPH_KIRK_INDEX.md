## Continuity anchors

- Kirk and Joseph remain distinct people with mutual, identity-continuous connection. Their eventual partnership is an earned literary direction, not a supernatural assignment.
- Both initiate consequential actions. Neither exists only to rescue, interpret, or validate the other.
- The four travelers choose their own routes. The city offers departures but does not assign them.
- Compare accounts by sequence and concrete detail. Preserve disagreement and uncertainty rather than forcing a single shared memory.
- The Procession can hold four accounts in one place without merging them.
- The Digitorium translates decisions into architecture and sensation; it is not established as a mind-reading device.
- The blank centre remains unentered. It is not assumed to be the source of the second heartbeat.
- The second heartbeat remains unresolved. Do not assign its source prematurely.
- The “YES JOEY” image is an associative story event seeded by a documented public post, not proof of private communication or participation by the real person named Joseph Prever.
