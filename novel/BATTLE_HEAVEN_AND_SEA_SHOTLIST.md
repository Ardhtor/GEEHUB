# BATTLE — HEAVEN & SEA / SHOT SEQUENCE

This sequence is subordinate to the novel. It exists to turn the canonical event into temporal production without replacing the literary text.

01 — BLACK WATER
Nearly black ocean. No horizon. Wind before image.

02 — THE SHIP
Kirk at the bow. Luke enters frame beside him. The ship is small against the water.

03 — HEAVEN DESCENDS
Cloud mass lowers until the sky appears to touch the ocean.

04 — THE MEN GO QUIET
Close human faces. No exposition. Their silence establishes that something has changed.

05 — SOMETHING BELOW
Water rises against the hull from underneath rather than from a wave.

06 — THE ORGANISM
A colossal back crosses beneath the ship. Ancient structures are visible along its surface.

07 — THE WATERLINE
Camera crosses the surface. Above: storm and ships. Below: inverted luminous heaven.

08 — RETURN TO KIRK
Kirk sees the second world and understands the production rule.

09 — THE SEAM
A vertical fracture opens in heaven.

10 — FIRST REVEAL
A column descends. The audience cannot yet determine whether it is architecture, mountain, or machine.

11 — FULL SCALE
The celestial structure becomes legible through human reference: windows, bridges, towers, impossible distances.

12 — JOSEPH
At the stern. Hand on mast. He watches the structure, then looks toward the rising sea fleet.

13 — THE FLEET
Ancient black ships break the ocean in sequence.

14 — THE COMMAND
Joseph: “Move.”

15 — THE TURN
The ship turns. The fleet turns with it.

16 — HEAVEN MOVES
The celestial formations begin converging toward the same center.

17 — SEA MOVES
The ocean itself appears to carry the fleet toward the opening.

18 — LUKE / KIRK
A brief look. No explanation.

19 — THE SECOND WORLD
The camera crosses the boundary.

20 — END FRAME
The men enter the impossible architecture. The battle continues behind them. Bells begin beneath the ocean.

The sequence must remain open-ended. The next generated discovery becomes the next canonical event.
