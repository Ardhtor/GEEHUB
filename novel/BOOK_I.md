# Book I — The Road of Seven Wells

**Status:** source map for an un-consolidated manuscript; not a complete reader-facing text.  
**Working title:** *The Road of Seven Wells*, as named in [The Three Books Moving at Once](THREE_BOOKS_IN_MOTION.md). The current environmental rewrite calls itself *The House of Open Screens* in its file heading. That title discrepancy remains open until the intended book title is confirmed.

## Why this file is an index

This file was an empty placeholder when inspected on 24 November 2026, despite being listed as Book I's main manuscript in the three-book index. It is now a source map so that the repository has a truthful entry point. No chapter text has been copied, merged, or promoted by this change.

## In-world naming status

The environmental rewrite no longer gives the future city an external workshop name. Its sign remains unmarked, and Luke and Joseph explicitly defer naming the place until they understand what it is. No final name is established in the current source candidate; the men must arrive at one through later lived experience and conversation.
