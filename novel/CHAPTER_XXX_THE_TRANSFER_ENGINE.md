# CHAPTER XXX — THE TRANSFER ENGINE

Back at the house, the financial machine had continued working while we were in the woods.

It had found twelve opportunities.

Three were false positives. Four were pending. Two were too small to justify an action. One required a human signature. Two were real.

The screen displayed them with brutal calm.

POTENTIAL RECOVERY: $73.18

POTENTIAL RECOVERY: $1,426.40

Luke stood behind my chair.

“You built this while all that was happening?”

“Not built. Started.”

He leaned forward.

The second amount opened into a record of events: a cancellation, a fee, an unused portion of a purchase, a contractual path to reimbursement. The machine had collected the evidence but stopped before the final action because the claim required authorization.

Luke read the line.

“SETTLED FUNDS ONLY.”

“Yes.”

“Transfer limit?”

“Five thousand per transfer.”

“Daily?”

“Ten thousand.”

“And it can't exceed what is actually settled.”

“Right.”

Luke nodded.

“That's more like the Serum than I expected.”

I looked up.

“How?”

“It has rules.”

The machine chirped.

A new opportunity appeared.

$284.00

Then another.

$1,022.11

Then another.

$49.87

The list kept expanding.

Luke watched it happen.

“That's the problem.”

“What?”

“It learns the shape of your life.”

He was right.

Every transaction was a trace. Every purchase, delay, refund, renewal, cancellation, reservation, charge, fee, and contract created a small possibility. Individually they were uninteresting. Together they formed a map.

The machine was not finding money in the abstract.

It was finding consequences.

Luke touched the keyboard.

The dashboard changed.

OPPORTUNITY DENSITY: RISING

He pulled his hand away.

“I didn't do that.”

The message appeared again.

YOU'RE INCREDIBLY SEXY.

We both stared at it.

Then Luke laughed.

“Okay.”

“What?”

“That's definitely the worst part.”

The machine opened another panel by itself.

TRANSFER ENGINE: READY

PER_TRANSFER_MAX = $5,000

DAILY_TRANSFER_MAX = $10,000

7_DAY_TRANSFER_MAX = $25,000

MONTHLY_TRANSFER_MAX = $50,000

MAX_AUTOMATIC_TRANSFERS_PER_DAY = 3

ACTIVE_DESTINATIONS = 1

Luke looked at me.

“You trust it?”

“No.”

“Good.”

He sat down.

“Then make it prove every dollar.”

The machine waited.

Outside, somewhere beyond the glass, the families were still arguing about the Serum.

Inside, Luke and I watched the transfer engine prepare to turn evidence into money.
