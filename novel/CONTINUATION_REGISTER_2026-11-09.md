## Muscle Myth — The Brotherhood of Giants

[The Brotherhood of Giants](../lore/THE_BROTHERHOOD_OF_GIANTS.md) establishes the Nephilim as brothers within the giant kindred and develops their shared dominion as a civilization of houses, halls, roads, craft, athletic culture, hospitality, and inherited maintenance. The opening book records the Firstborn, the Stone Table, the Houses of Dominion, the Office of Measure, the Age of Convergence, and the Line of Keeping. Five opening chapters dramatize the Nephilim's return, the repair of their ancestral hall, a multi-house contest, the construction of a road through a living valley, and the negotiations after the feast. The work is a GEEHUB mythic interpretation, not a claim to settle historical or religious questions about Nephilim traditions. The coastal shipyard thread has advanced in [The Coastal Shipyard](../lore/THE_COASTAL_SHIPYARD.md), a supplementary mathematical-historical treatise. It establishes the River Aven harbour's lower landing, middle cargo quay, and upper working terrace; the first deep-water test vessel, *The Measure of Dawn*; a repair quay and harbour ledger; distributed crew responsibilities; and the coastal house's technical relationship with inland engineers. The first controlled voyage returns after detecting a cross-current and a vibrating forward fitting. A later storm exposes a stores-register discrepancy, prompting a revised replacement procedure. The full deep-water trial, first winter, southern timber contribution, and Saltline ports remain open. The other active threads are the southern escarpment halls, the multi-house athletic festival, and the surveyed road beyond the northern ranges.

The public reference sources discussed in the social-feed work remain distinct from the DeviantArt collection. A post, video, or saved artwork can inspire a GEEHUB study without becoming a factual biography of the person depicted or a licence to reproduce an artist's work.
