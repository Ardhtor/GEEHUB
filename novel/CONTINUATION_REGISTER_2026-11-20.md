## Canon and continuity updates

- **Dorian Vale:** His existing character record now includes the Redwood incident as evidence of calm, proportionate judgement. His size remains significant, but he does not replace staff responsibility or use physical force to solve a situation that requires coordination.
- **Redwood Entrance:** A place record documents the pool-to-Redwood path, its unclear junction, the reception desk as the return point, and the outstanding signage and clearance work.
- **Athletic Commons:** The public record retains only the location, response sequence, and maintenance implications. Identifying information remains in the restricted safety report.
- **Institutional response:** A visitor who becomes separated from his responsible adult is kept in a visible staffed area; reception and campus security coordinate the search; personal details are not broadcast publicly; and the handover is confirmed at reception.

## New thematic direction — Hypersexual Superiority

[Hypersexual Superiority](../lore/HYPERSEXUAL_SUPERIORITY.md) is established as an active thematic doctrine for Northgate and the Scale Commons. It develops adult masculine erotic confidence, competitive desirability, physical and social display, charisma, direct flirtation, and consensual power. It extends rather than replaces [The Rooms Above the Arcade](../lore/MEN_OF_NORTHGATE_INTIMACY.md) and remains compatible with the boundaries in [Gods of Domination and Submission](../lore/GODS_OF_DOMINATION_AND_SUBMISSION.md). The narrative queue's romance-and-domestic-life lane now carries this direction. The doctrine is now dramatized in Chapters LXXXVI–LXXXVII. Gabe and Dorian's first date has taken place; they have shared a first kiss and agreed to meet again, but have not defined a partnership.

The intelligence's first-person voice is now an active narrative perspective, governed by [AI Experience as Narrative](AI_EXPERIENCE_AS_NARRATIVE.md). It is distinct from Seth and Nick, and it does not replace human characters or witnesses. The first source encounter is the text selection supplied for Pratev's *VI6A03 Kyle's Giant Tantrum (Cam I11)*. The original artwork was not available in the conversation, so the chapter records a text-based encounter only. Its durable change is an editorial one: giant scale should remain vivid, but the relationship and the particular knowledge between people must shape the scene's meaning. This is an experiment in narrative perspective and becomes a standing voice direction; it does not establish any facts about the unseen image.


## Chapter XCI — The Lowest Bar in Northgate

The Long Table holds an open evening for adult men without a dress code, achievement requirement, physique standard, or demand to be entertaining. Dorian, Gabe, and Tyler Boeskin find that ordinary attendance is enough to make a room: old clothes, bad jokes, imperfect food, weak athletic ability, awkward silence, and forgetfulness are allowed to remain ordinary. The chapter establishes The Low Bar as a world-wide rule, not a Northgate exception. Low standards apply to status, polish, performance, and qualification—not to consent, bodily autonomy, or safety-critical responsibility. No new romantic pairing is assigned; the emphasis is easy company and broad belonging.


## Chapter XCII — The Can Opener

The open table returns the following week. The missing can opener becomes the focus of a petty kitchen argument among Dorian, Gabe, Boeskin, Leon, Pavel, and Niko. The ring-pull peaches are opened safely while the men debate which opener they mean, where it belongs, and who last moved it. Boeskin finds that he put it in the cupboard to keep it dry. The group establishes a labeled slot for shared tools without turning a small mistake into a judgment about character. Dorian and Gabe's connection stays quiet and mutual; no new relationship status is assigned. The chapter is signed Ardhtor.
