## Canon and continuity updates

- **Dorian Vale:** His existing character record now includes the Redwood incident as evidence of calm, proportionate judgement. His size remains significant, but he does not replace staff responsibility or use physical force to solve a situation that requires coordination.
- **Redwood Entrance:** A place record documents the pool-to-Redwood path, its unclear junction, the reception desk as the return point, and the outstanding signage and clearance work.
- **Athletic Commons:** The public record retains only the location, response sequence, and maintenance implications. Identifying information remains in the restricted safety report.
- **Institutional response:** A visitor who becomes separated from his responsible adult is kept in a visible staffed area; reception and campus security coordinate the search; personal details are not broadcast publicly; and the handover is confirmed at reception.

## Open work

The temporary directional arrow remains installed. A two-sided paper mock-up has been tested from both approaches, and its directional alignment has been corrected; the permanent sign drawing, wording approval, manufacture, installation, and final two-direction check remain open. The floor-marking durability test also remains open. The seating clearance has been measured and the display stand moved; the clear route should be checked again during normal peak use.

The western bridge remains closed pending its engineering review. Dorian and Gabe have become more familiar through their shared work at the Commons, but they are not yet established as close friends; the relationship remains open to further development. The two Redwood chapters continue the campus branch without replacing the open bridge and friendship threads.
