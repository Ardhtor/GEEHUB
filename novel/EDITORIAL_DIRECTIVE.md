# GEEHUB Novel Editorial Directive

Revise the existing manuscript as a living novel, not as a collection of scene notes.

## Prose

Use long, substantial paragraphs throughout. Do not reduce scenes to one-sentence beats, screenplay-like fragments, or object/action inventories. Paragraphs should carry atmosphere, movement, memory, physical sensation, place, character presence, thought, and consequence together.

Avoid object-oriented narration: do not make the prose proceed by naming an object and immediately assigning it an action or symbolic function. Objects may appear, but description should move freely through the surrounding world and the characters' perception of it. Let rooms, weather, bodies, distance, light, sound, history, memory, and emotion intermingle without forcing each detail into a discrete narrative unit.

Prefer expansive descriptive passages and sustained scene movement. Dialogue should emerge from the prose rather than interrupt it every few lines. Preserve the strange mythology, but allow it to breathe through concrete physical experience.

## Bill

Bill requires especially verbose, personal, digressive prose. Give him history, humor, habits, opinions, memories, contradictions, stories inside stories, and an unmistakable voice. His importance as keeper of continuity should emerge through what he notices and remembers rather than through repeated exposition. Bill I should feel like a major literary section, not a character profile.

## Continuity

Preserve existing canon and expand it rather than replacing it with summaries. Joseph ultimately ends up with Kirk; develop that trajectory through accumulated scenes and consequences. Preserve Luke, Tyler, Kirk, Joseph, Bill, the Sacred Fountain, the wells, the Serum, the house, Big Daddy's Voice, the first man, and the existing artifact relationships.

## Revision rule

When expanding an existing chapter, retain its factual events and important lines where possible, but surround them with substantially richer prose, transitions, description, character interiority, history, and consequence. Chapters should become longer as they progress. The manuscript should feel increasingly inhabited rather than increasingly compressed.

Core engine: WHAT IS WRONG -> WHAT DOES IT PRODUCE -> WHAT HAS CHANGED.
