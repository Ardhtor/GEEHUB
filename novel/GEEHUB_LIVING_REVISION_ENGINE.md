# GEEHUB — LIVING REVISION ENGINE

## Purpose

Revise the hub by accumulation, not replacement. Ordinary scenes, unfinished chapters, artifacts, video, geography, character memory, and unresolved details remain inputs. The prose is a continuous environmental broadcast, narrated in a podcast-style exchange by Seth Feroce and Nick Mercx.

The global authority is [../docs/GLOBAL_ENVIRONMENTAL_BROADCAST_REVISION.md](../docs/GLOBAL_ENVIRONMENTAL_BROADCAST_REVISION.md).

## Paragraph engine

The paragraph engine now defaults to long-form continuity throughout the hub. Compose substantial paragraphs that carry a complete movement of thought or experience, allowing setting, action, bodily presence, memory, and consequence to remain connected. Prefer several developed sentences over repeated short lines or isolated dramatic fragments. A paragraph break should mark a real shift in attention, location, subject, or environmental condition; use it to carry the reader into the next part of the experience. Apply this standard to chapters, lore, commentary, production notes, descriptions, and generated prose, while preserving functional formatting for code, data, metadata, lists, and procedural instructions.



Every paragraph break, line break, and scene transition introduces or deepens non-plot information: terrain, architecture, weather, lighting, atmosphere, spatial depth, material, sound, temperature, distance, machinery, or the physical presence of people within a place. Line breaks are environmental descriptors, not plot beats.

Seth and Nick announce what is present, compare observations, react to the scale of a place, and make the world feel encountered rather than explained. Use substantial, embodied prose rather than a list of setting notes. Action stays legible, but the environment carries the transitions.

## Continuity

Never discard canon merely to make room for new prose. Preserve chronology, established dialogue, character identity, relationships, and source provenance. Environmental details can accumulate memory across scenes, but do not invent consequential events or quietly promote experiments into canon. Serum is established but non-central lore; never use it as the default explanation for change, growth, memory, or continuity.

## Narrative ease of production

Production is a first-class form of continuity. A project should carry its own passage from encounter to artifact: source, observation, interpretation, experiment, revision, verification, and release. These are not disconnected administrative statuses; they are the history of how the work became what it is. Keep the path navigable in both directions. From an artifact, a reader can return to its source and decisions; from a source, a maker can see what has been derived and what remains possible.

When composing new prose, let the practical work enter naturally through the scene. A character can make a measurement, discover a structural problem, move a piece of furniture, test a material, inspect a frame, or revise an object. The action should be legible as production and consequential within the world. Do not pause the story to explain a workflow that can be embodied in what people do. Conversely, do not hide technical facts inside atmosphere: dimensions, versions, file identities, test outcomes, and unresolved states must remain precise where they matter.

Every production pass leaves a residue record: source reference; user direction; observed facts; interpretation; files or assets changed; before and after state; verification performed; open questions; and the next operation made possible. Keep this record concise and machine-readable where the runtime supports it, with a human-readable account attached. If persistence is not implemented, say so. Do not simulate a completed ledger by writing as though a future system already exists.

The practical test is simple: can someone arrive at the project midway through its life, understand what is here, see why it took this form, and continue without first reconstructing the whole history from chat? If not, the production structure is not yet carrying enough of the narrative.
