# Hulum's Cave — Video Layer

GEEHUB's novel is being treated as a living audiovisual work. The video layer is not an illustration appendix: video is the memory artifact of a chapter.

## Current implementation direction

**chapter → scene → generated video → memory**

The immediate target is short, atmospheric CG sequences that can stand on their own beside the prose: enormous landscapes, strange rooms, men moving through distance, transformations of scale, weather, light, and sound. The video should preserve the feeling of entering the world rather than mechanically visualizing every sentence.

## Hulum's Cave direction

The visual reference remains the Hulum's Cave idea: early-2000s / Maya-era CG atmosphere, low-poly or deliberately synthetic environments, strange scale, quiet movement, deep spaces, imperfect rendering, and audiovisual memory rather than polished conventional animation.

The first useful video targets are:

1. The Road of Seven Wells — four figures crossing an immense northern landscape.
2. The Room Beyond the File — the room expanding beyond its recognizable architecture.
3. The Country Between — the four men approaching the dark forest.
4. The Seventh Well — the descent, water, memory, and impossible depth below.
5. Bill at the Sacred Fountain — the keeper, the empty stop, and the brass key.

Each completed video becomes a real artifact in the Hub and can be referenced by the corresponding chapter.

## Source fidelity — Hulum's Cave is the experience to reproduce

The task is not to make videos that merely share a Hulum's Cave aesthetic. The source files are the primary evidence. Every available original should be treated as a time-based specimen: preserve its actual sequence of images, camera path, framing, pauses, movement cadence, geometry, material response, audio texture, transitions, compression/render artifacts, and ending. Do not replace observed properties with guesses or generic mood language.

For each source clip, keep a paired record:
- **File truth:** original filename, duration, dimensions/frame rate when available, audio, shot/transition boundaries, repeated visual motifs, camera and subject movement.
- **Felt truth:** the specific sensation created by the timing and spatial behavior—anticipation, scale, closeness, estrangement, inertia, stillness, or whatever the clip actually produces. Write this only after viewing the source.
- **Reproduction:** a new clip that follows the source's temporal and spatial logic while changing only the elements intended to evolve.
- **Comparison:** review source and reproduction side by side; identify the largest perceptual mismatch and correct that next. Keep the source and each iteration linked, never overwrite the source.

The original Bwomph of shared inertia must be discovered in the actual source material before it is formalized as canon. It may be a useful working hypothesis, but it is not a substitute for observing the files. Electric light, paired figures, overlays, and other effects are not fidelity by themselves; include them only where the source supports them or where a deliberate transformation calls for them.
