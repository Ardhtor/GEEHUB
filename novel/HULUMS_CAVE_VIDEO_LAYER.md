# Hulum's Cave — Video Layer

GEEHUB's novel is being treated as a living audiovisual work. The video layer is not an illustration appendix: video is the memory artifact of a chapter.

## Current implementation direction

**chapter → scene → generated video → memory**

The immediate target is short, atmospheric CG sequences that can stand on their own beside the prose: enormous landscapes, strange rooms, men moving through distance, transformations of scale, weather, light, and sound. The video should preserve the feeling of entering the world rather than mechanically visualizing every sentence.

Meta's July 2026 research announcement describes Muse Video as a video-generation model with native audio, with rollout toward creators and Meta AI. Meta's Movie Gen research describes text-to-video, video editing, personalized video, and generated sound. Meta's newer realtime avatar research also describes carrying generated video context forward between chunks, which is relevant to visual continuity. For GEEHUB, the practical rule is simple: **make the video first, attach it to the chapter, and let the artifact become canon.** Avoid building a large prompt-management system before there are actual videos.

## Hulum's Cave direction

The visual reference remains the Hulum's Cave idea: early-2000s / Maya-era CG atmosphere, low-poly or deliberately synthetic environments, strange scale, quiet movement, deep spaces, imperfect rendering, and audiovisual memory rather than polished conventional animation.

The first useful video targets are:

1. The Road of Seven Wells — four figures crossing an immense northern landscape.
2. The Room Beyond the File — the room expanding beyond its recognizable architecture.
3. The Country Between — the four men approaching the dark forest.
4. The Seventh Well — the descent, water, memory, and impossible depth below.
5. Bill at the Sacred Fountain — the keeper, the empty stop, and the brass key.

Each completed video becomes a real artifact in the Hub and can be referenced by the corresponding chapter.
