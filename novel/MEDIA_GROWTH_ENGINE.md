# GEEHUB — MEDIA GROWTH ENGINE

The book does not have to become only a book.

A resolved or unresolved scene may grow into another medium:

`PROSE → IMAGE → SOUND → VIDEO → WEB SCENE → ARTIFACT → PROSE`

The medium is chosen by what the scene wants to preserve.

- Image: preserve a face, place, composition, object, or instant.
- Sound: preserve atmosphere, voice, distance, weather, machinery, silence.
- Video: preserve movement, duration, choreography, scale, and ordinary time.
- Web scene: preserve exploration and spatial relationships.
- Artifact: preserve a physical-feeling trace such as a map, transcript, photograph, receipt, notebook page, sign, ticket, or production record.
- Prose: preserve interiority, memory, history, and things that cannot otherwise be seen.

The alternative-media pass should never become a poster generator. It should create evidence from the world.

For each useful scene:

`WHAT IS MISSING → CHOOSE THE MEDIUM THAT CAN HOLD IT → MAKE THE ARTIFACT → RETURN ITS NEW INFORMATION TO THE WORLD`

The artifact then becomes part of canon and may generate the next scene.

For the post-book cycle, favor small media: a short driving sequence, a room recording, a still from an afternoon, ambient rain, a notebook scan, a crude map, a recorded conversation, a webpage representing one room, or a sequence of images showing an ordinary day changing.

The purpose is not to illustrate the prose. The purpose is to make the world exist in more than one form.
