# THE VOID BETWEEN MEN

## Mystery

What qualities make MEN?

The working premise is not that masculinity has already been defined. The experience is an instrument for discovering what exists in the shared space between men.

## First visual state

A group of distinct adult men occupies a dark redwood clearing. Each remains visibly individual: body, posture, age, clothing, history, attention.

Between them, an invisible structure becomes visible.

It is not another character. It is a field produced by relationship: recognition, rivalry, friendship, responsibility, inheritance, restraint, vulnerability, craft, agency, and becoming.

## Questions it is allowed to generate

What is shared by men without being identical between them?

What appears only when one man is witnessed by another?

Which qualities are bodily, which are relational, and which are inherited?

What does strength mean when restraint is possible?

What does vulnerability look like between men?

What survives when rivalry becomes recognition?

What does a group of men create that no individual man contains?
