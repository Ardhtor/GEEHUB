# Hulum's Cave — Dim Path Modular Kit

A generated, modular environment asset pack for the lowered and dimmed approach path in the Cave. It is deliberately a component kit rather than a complete scene: the base path, broken pavers, left and right rock shoulders, shallow water seams and low mist cards can be separated, repositioned, replaced or extended independently.

The OBJ uses metres, with the near entrance at the origin and the path receding along +Z. Import `dim_path.obj` into Blender or Maya and retain the object groups/material assignments. The accompanying MTL supplies a dark basalt/slate palette, restrained water reflectance and a translucent mist material. Keep the overall exposure low; the threshold should remain the brightest point in the composition. Water should read as occasional cold glints rather than a bright illuminated strip.

This is a first-pass geometry kit, not a final photoreal render or a claim that the original video has been reproduced. Treat it as experimental GEEHUB material until it has been tested under the Cave's camera and lighting.
