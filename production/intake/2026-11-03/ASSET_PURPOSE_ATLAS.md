## Creation plan

### A. Luke / embodied-scale sequence
Use `lukemurpu` as the identity candidate; the correction frame as the review constraint; Agata as a physique and silhouette reference; the Oct 30 generated image as a candidate prior pass. Build a baseline record and one localized growth-pass brief. Keep stage-four presentation, recognizable face, limb ratios, natural movement, skin and fabric realism. Do not promote an unreviewed generated frame to baseline.

### B. Diego II sequence
Treat both Diego II videos as a paired source set. Compare them before deduplication. Extract a neutral character sheet: silhouette, proportions, gait, camera distance, pose changes, lighting and scene transitions. Build a separate character lane; do not merge Diego with Luke or Joseph.

### C. Growth-animation study
Use the StoreRoom cartoon clip as a transformation-grammar reference. Break it into beats: initial state, trigger, visible delta, stress/reaction, resulting state. Borrow the legibility of the progression, not the exact scene or identity.

### D. Social-motion library
Group the SaveTwitter, SnapVid and WhatsApp captures as motion references. Each receives source provenance, dimensions, duration, representative frame times, camera/motion notes and a possible shot role. The opaque filenames stay unchanged; descriptive labels live in manifests.

### E. Joseph / narrative and release lane
The Joseph FOMO markdown becomes a reusable release-copy engine, separate from the Joseph–Kirk fiction. The Sword DOCX is a text-source candidate pending extraction. Neither is treated as evidence of any real person's private intent or participation. The Joseph–Kirk story remains in its own experimental Veyrthalis branch.

### F. GEEHUB production instrument
The Paper spec is the product contract. The Lua script is a Resolve-side audit and safe setup utility, not the video-generation engine. Next concrete build object: a source manifest and shot cards that can be imported into the real editing/generation pipeline.

## Intake rules

- Source originals are immutable; derived frames and edits go in new output paths.
- Keep opaque filenames as source IDs and add human-readable aliases in metadata.
- Never infer a real person's identity, consent, intention or private relationship from a reference file.
- Separate observed facts, filename-based guesses and creative proposals.
- A reference can inform multiple projects, but each project gets its own derived shot/state record.
- No paid generation job is launched without showing its model, duration, resolution and credit cost and receiving explicit approval.
- A prompt, storyboard, still sequence or mock timeline is not a finished generated video.
