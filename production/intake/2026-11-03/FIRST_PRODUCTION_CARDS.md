# GEEHUB Intake — First Production Cards

These cards turn the asset atlas into executable editorial work. They are briefs, not claims that the footage has already been inspected or rendered.

## INTAKE-3333 — newest source
**Source:** `/3333.mp4`  
**Purpose:** identify the new upload's subject, action and visual language before assigning it to a project.  
**Pass:** inspect opening, midpoint and ending frames; note duration, dimensions, frame rate, camera movement, subject movement, lighting and any continuity-relevant details.  
**Output:** `3333_source_review.json`, 3–8 selected frame references, and a recommendation for the correct project lane.  
**Decision gate:** no character assignment until the subject is visually confirmed.
