# Kirk's Lineage — Generation 01

**Status:** production baseline · **Cohort:** 20 fictional adult men · **Generation:** 01 · **Created:** 2026-11-23

Generation 01 is the founding population of Kirk's fictional generative lineage. A common generative substrate explains their shared origin; it does not make them copies, presets, or a collective personality. Every man has a stable face, body silhouette, specialization, temperament, competence, social position, and growth history. Later generations may inherit selected traits, skills, habits, and relationships, but each successor must reinterpret what he receives.

All members are fictional adults aged 24–41. Height and physique descriptions are production baselines. No real-person likeness is assigned. The structured source of truth is [generation-01.json](./generation-01.json); individual continuity records are stored in [characters/](./characters/).

## Production status

The character identities, visual anchors, social graph, growth trajectories, and modular model specifications are established as a production baseline. Reference-sheet and turnaround requirements are specified, but rendered image sheets, rigged meshes, animation clips, and final lighting/material turntables remain separate deliverables and must not be marked complete until generated and reviewed.

## Acceptance gates

1. Exactly 20 unique adult characters; all identity and production fields populated.
2. Every character has a distinct face, silhouette anchors, specialization, personality, competence, movement signature, and growth trajectory.
3. All relationship references resolve to a member of Generation 01.
4. Each record specifies front/side/back turnarounds, four signature poses, and separable modular assets.
5. Render review confirms facial identity and proportions remain stable across views and growth states.
6. Asset provenance and review results are recorded before promotion to final production.

## Continuity and provenance

Generation 01 is the canon baseline. New designs that alter a face, proportions, role, or relationship must be recorded as a versioned change, not silently substituted. Every visual asset or mesh must link to the character ID, generation, prompt version, and parent asset IDs.
