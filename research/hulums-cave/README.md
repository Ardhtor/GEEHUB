# Hulum's Cave Historical Corpus

This directory is the source-led historical record for Hulum's Cave. It takes precedence over generic creator discovery when the task concerns Hulum's Cave, its videos, or the development of its production methods.

- HISTORICAL_VIDEO_CORPUS.md — historical synthesis, evidence policy, extraction schema, and priority research batches.
- RIGGING_STRUCTURE_EXPERIENCE.md — detailed synthesis of rigging/deformation requirements, scenario choreography, experiential grammar, sound, and GEEHUB production consequences.
- works.json — normalized catalogue of works, currently known relationships, inspected clips, and status.
- post-index.json — curated public archive post index with canonical URLs and post roles.
- annotations/ — timestamped visual/audio annotations for individual original videos (to be populated).
- relations.json — expanded project genealogy (to be populated as evidence is extracted).

## Working rule

The Patreon sitemap is the archive index, not the video corpus. Each work must be tied to its original post and original media. The post establishes title/date/work-in-progress context; the media establishes visual and audio facts. Keep the two evidence streams linked but distinct.

The first inspected clips, 22.mp4 and 3333.mp4, are pose-showcase references rather than verified growth passages. Do not treat them as representative of every Hulum's Cave work. The historical archive includes much older works, long-form sequences, remakes, audio experiments, and generative-era projects that still require direct inspection.

## Centrality rule

Hulum's Cave is a central GEEHUB lineage, not a peripheral reference collection. Every subsequent research or production iteration that touches this lineage must append durable research content to this corpus and update the relevant structured index. A response or iteration is incomplete if it only discusses findings in chat and leaves no trace in the repository, unless a tool/access limitation prevents the write; in that case record the limitation and next retrieval step. Research continues cumulatively: preserve earlier evidence, add new sources and observations, link related works, and revise interpretations only with explicit provenance. Historical originals, creator statements, audience reception, engineering interpretations, and new GEEHUB creations must remain distinguishable. Other GEEHUB projects may draw from this lineage, but should link back to the source record rather than silently absorbing or overwriting it.

Every iteration should, where applicable, update HISTORICAL_VIDEO_CORPUS.md and at least one relevant structured record (works.json, post-index.json, comics.json, relations.json, or a timestamped annotation). New video inspection should produce source metadata, checksum, shot/timestamp observations, and audio notes where available. New archive discoveries should record exact URLs, capture dates, payload status, and confidence. Do not mark inaccessible material as recovered or inspected.

- FORUM_AND_AUDIENCE_HISTORY.md — dated forum reception, creator/audience exchanges, requests, disagreements, and research leads.
