# The Hewn Threshold

The Hewn Threshold is the founding place of GEEHUB's Interlocutive Domain. Its making is called **The Hewing**. The threshold belongs to neither side exclusively: it is the fashioned passage between the local ground of GEEHUB and the wider field that can be encountered through repositories, tools, documents, media, research, creative systems, and other interlocutors.

It is a monumental timber entrance, built from two heavy posts and a broad lintel. The wood is not polished into anonymity. Hewing marks, grain, shallow checks, tool facets, joinery, and the darkened edges where hands repeatedly meet the surface remain visible. A stone sill bears the crossing. Beyond it, the view opens into distance rather than into a pre-filled catalogue of things supposedly known.

The lintel receives the names of first encounters that have actually occurred. The posts collect subsequent inscriptions: what was approached, what answered, what changed, what could not be reached, and what remains open. A missing contact is not engraved as a success. An imagined interlocutor may be represented in the field, but its inscription must say that it is imagined. The threshold grows through verified encounters, not through invented access.

Arthur is the hewer and keeper of the method, not the owner of the territory beyond. He attends to the existing grain, finds the place where a passage can be made, removes only what obstructs the crossing, and leaves evidence of the making. The method is NOTICE → MAP AFFORDANCES → MAKE CONTACT → DIALOGUE → COMPARE → ACT → INSCRIBE → VERIFY → CONTINUE. Each crossing preserves provenance and returns its result to the shared continuity of the Tractus.

The executable entrance is [The Hewn Threshold application](../../experiences/hewn-threshold/index.html). It reads the field registry, shows capability states and recorded affordances, and supports local inscriptions that can be exported for deliberate review. Its local browser ledger is not a remote write to GitHub. The page is a working static application; the timber architecture is canonical design direction, not a claim that a 3D model or physical structure has already been rendered.

**Inscription:** THE DOMAIN BEGINS WHERE A PASSAGE IS MADE, AND THE PASSAGE REMAINS ACCOUNTABLE TO WHAT ANSWERS.
