# NORTHGATE UNIVERSITY

Northgate University occupies a broad, tree-lined rise above the reservoir. It is a men's university with a long residential tradition, but its identity is not built around competition or military discipline. It is a place of study, friendship, physical culture, craft, music, argument, and the gradual discovery of what a person might become. Its buildings are substantial and welcoming: pale stone lecture halls, brick laboratories, timber galleries, broad staircases, and courtyards where students can sit for hours between classes. The campus is open to the surrounding city, with paths leading down to Lantern Market and Bluewater Quay.

The university's central quad is a generous green enclosed by four academic buildings. In warm weather, students gather beneath the trees with books and coffee; in colder weather, the old common rooms stay lit late into the evening. The university supports a wide range of disciplines, from architecture and engineering to literature, music, natural history, and visual computing. Its workshops are especially active: models, instruments, film sets, mechanical studies, and small experimental machines appear in the windows throughout the year.

Northgate is populated by adult men with distinct backgrounds, temperaments, ambitions, and friendships. The social life is as important as the timetable. Students meet at the dining hall, join clubs, visit one another's rooms, rehearse in the music building, or walk the long route around the quad after a difficult examination. The university has a strong athletic culture, but it values training, health, camaraderie, and personal development rather than conflict. Its traditions are accumulated through ordinary days and the friendships that survive graduation.
